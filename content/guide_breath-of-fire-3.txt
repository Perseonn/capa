@title: Breath of Fire III
@author: Perseonn Balthasaar
@updated: 2025-04-06

Breath of Fire III is an RPG made by Capcom for PlayStation. This is the second RPG I've ever played and have the least modification during my playthrough. Of all the Breath of Fire I've played (being III, IV and V), I like this one most due to the custom dragon system and the storyline.
