+++ About DQ Units
The DQ Units refers to the ★13 units dropped on Divide Quest - Photoner Assault. On their own, they are weak, but when you equip 3 (doesn't matter which), you'll gain 200 HP, 20 PP, 150 [[span class="icon triple stat-aatk"]]ATK[[/span]]. All rears are, more less have same stat: 10 HP, 1PP and 10 [[span class="icon triple stat-aatk"]]ATK[[/span]]. The arms and legs, however, have varied stat. For attacking purpose, the 40 ATK ones are desirable and they drop relatively often. The 50 and 80 ATK ones, are however, much rarer.
