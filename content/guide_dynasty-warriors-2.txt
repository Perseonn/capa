Unlike [[[guide:dynastywarriors | Dynasty Warriors]]] which was a fighting game Dynasty Warriors 2 was the start of the 1 vs 100 Musou style of game play you are familiar with nowadays. There is not much to unlock or cover with Dynasty Warriors 2 so this guide encompasses all you can do in it.

+ Musou Mode
All characters go through the same 5 stages in Musou Mode regardless of who you pick, they all share the same first two stages that being Yellow Turban Rebellion & The Battle at Hu Lao Gate, the remaining three are based on which kingdom they belong to. Characters counted as Others have no Musou Mode and can only be used in Free Mode.

||~ Kingdom ||~ Stage 3 ||~ Stage 4 ||~ Stage 5 ||
|| Shu || The Battle of Chang Ban || The Battle at Yi Ling || The Battle at Wu Zhang Plains ||
|| Wei || The Battle at Guan Du || The Battle at Chi Bi || The Battle at Wu Zhang Plains ||
|| Wu || The Battle at Chi Bi || The Battle at He Fei || The Battle at Yi Ling ||

+ Dim Sum
All stages have a Dim Sum which increases max Life & Musou by 10, certain sides also have additional Dim Sum drops by defeating certain generals for even more increases. These are located in pots outside of the general drops.

++ Yellow Turban Rebellion
After Cheng Yuanzi is defeated go through the gate and head North until you can climb the wall, it is located at the Northern end of the wall.
Yellow Turbans: Liu Bei, Sun Jian, Cao Cao.

+ Luminous Sword
This is a special sword drop which gives a higher than normal Attack increase at +10 regardless of the difficulty while others would go as high as +8, this sword is found in three instances.

The Battle of Chang Ban (Liu Bei's Forces): Xiahou En.
The Battle of Chang Ban (Cao Cao's Forces): Zhao Yun.
The Battle at He Fei (Sun Quan's Forces): Zhang Liao.
