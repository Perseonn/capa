Samurai Warriors 5 is the fifth mainline Samurai Warriors game and the first time where the series has had a major overhaul to its visual style as well as being the only entry in this era, this guide will go over character unlocking, stage objectives and where to go for the Rare Weapons.

This will list the stages and which characters you are able to choose for those battles and any who may come in mid battle to be a partner, characters with their name **in bold** have an objective they are needed for.

Playable: Nobunaga Oda

++++ 1-4 Battle of Muraki Garrison

++++ 1-5 Battle of Nagara River

# **The Lady and the Hostage** - Defeat Sena and Ieyasu Tokugawa to reach the retreat point.

# **Father’s Ruin (Bonus)** – Defeat Yoshitatsu Saitō.
# **The Strategist’s Betrayal (Bonus)** – Defeat Hanbei Takenaka.
# **What He Left Behind (Bonus)** – Defeat Motonobu Okabe and Yasutomo Asahina to prevent Nō from being taken out.
# **The Supreme Ruler of Tōkai (Special)** – Defeat Yoshimoto Imagawa. (2 minutes)
+++ Chapter 2 The Vow

Playable: Nobunaga Oda, **Hideyoshi Hashiba**, Nō, **Toshiie Maeda**

# **Destroying the Front Line** - Defeat the enemy troops to advance the Nobunaga army’s front line. (150)
# **Blazing a Trail** - Defeat Torazō Ōwaki and Heishirō Kanbe to clear the back road.
# **Striking at the Enemy’s Morale** - Defeat the commanding officer, Hidesada Hayashi.
# **A Sister’s Wish** - Defeat Oichi to clear a path to Suemori Castle.
# **A Demon of a Dead-End** - Defeat Katsuie Shibata.
# **A Foothold for Victory (Bonus)** – Defeat Shingo Tsunoda and Sukenojō Kamata to capture the Central Western Garrison and Northeastern Garrison.
# **Where, Oh Where Is the Back Road? (Hideyoshi Hashiba Bonus)** - Hideyoshi Hashiba must reach the back road.
# **Breaking through the Center (Toshiie Maeda Bonus)** – Toshiie Maeda must defeat the enemy troops in the Central Garrison to break through the center of their formation. (100)
# **Toshiie’s Assault (Bonus)** – Defeat Mimasakanokami Hayashi to clear the central path.
# **Mighty Spear Mataza (Special)** – Defeat Jūzō Hashimoto and Sakyōnoshin Tomino. [Approach after Kurando Tsuzuki and Matajirō Yamaguchi are defeated opening the Central Garrison, Toshiie must not be a chosen character.]
++++ 2-^2 Battle of Okehazama
Playable: **Nobunaga Oda** | Toshiie Maeda (mid stage)
# **Ambush on Dengakuhazama** - Defeat Tadatsugu Sakai, Ienari Ishikawa, and Tadazane Honda to ambush Dengakuhazama.
# **The Anticipated Ambush** - Defeat Hanzō Hattori and his allies.
# **The Leader of the Tokugawa Clan** - Defeat Ieyasu Tokugawa. 
# **The Comeback** - Defeat Sena and her allies to prevent Katsuie Shibata, Hideyoshi Hashiba, and Oichi from being taken out.
# **Fools Don’t Know When to Quit** - Attack enemy troops until the preparations are complete. (150)
# **The Tokugawa Clan’s Path** - Defeat Sena.
# **Reclaiming the Garrison (Bonus)** – Defeat Naomori Ii and Yasutomo Asahina to recapture Washizu Garrison and Marune garrison.
# **The Tokugawa Army Supply Soldiers (Bonus)** - Defeat all the Supply Captains to cut off the Imagawa Army’s supply lines.
# **The Successor to Samonji (Nobunaga Oda Bonus)** - Nobunaga Oda must defeat Yoshimoto Imagawa with a Musou Attack.
# **To Command the Imagawa (Special)** –Defeat Motonobu Okabe.
++++ 2-^3 Battle of Inabayama Castle
Playable: Nobunaga Oda, **Nō**, Toshiie Maeda, Katsuie Shibata, Oichi
# **Forward March to Sunomata Castle** - Defeat Yoshitaka Endō and Masanari Nonomura to advance toward Sunomata Castle.
# **Sunomata Castle Must Not Fall** - Prevent Sandayū Momochi from being defeated until the allied officers arrive. (2 minutes)
# **Yoshitatsu Struggles** - Defeat Mitsuharu Fuwa and his allies.

# **Join Us, My Friend!** - Defeat Hanbei Takenaka.
# **Stopping the Saitō  Army Reinforcements (Bonus)** – Defeat Hironari Hineno to prevent the arrival of enemy reinforcements.
# **To Strike from Behind (Bonus)** - Defeat Yorimoto Toki and Naomitsu Takegoshi to prevent Nobumori Sakuma from being taken out.
# **Farewell to the Past (Nō Bonus)** – Nō must defeat Yoshitatsu Saitō with a Musou Attack.
# **A Wavering Heart (Special)** - Defeat the surrounding enemy troops except for Ittetsu Inaba. (100) [Approach him near the start of the battle to hear him doubting Yoshitatsu to trigger.]
++++ 2-v1 Mayhem in Mikawa
Playable: (Main only) **Ieyasu Tokugawa** | (Partner only) Tadakatsu Honda, Hanzō Hattori.
# **To Crush a Rebellion** - Defeat Nobumoto Mizuno, and his allies, and prevent Tadayo Ōkubo and his allies from being taken out.
# **The Pride of the Men of Mikawa** - Defeat Sadayoshi Okudaira and Jinjirō Matsudira without taking out the peasant leaders.
# **Recurring Rebellion** - Defeat Sadakatsu Okudaira and Chikanori Matsudaira to crush the rebellion.
# **Lady Sena’s Rescue** - Defeat Hideyoshi Hashiba, Yoshinari Mori and Nobushige Suzuki, and prevent Sena from being taken out.
# **Budding Romance** - Work with Sena to defeat Katsuie Shibata.
# **Attack of the Wanderers (Bonus)** - Prevent any wanderers from infiltrating Okazaki Castle.
# **The Wanderers Return (Bonus)** - Defeat all wanderers to clear a path to the Southern Garrison.
# **The Demon Shibata and the Mighty Men of Mikawa (Ieyasu Tokugawa Bonus)** - Ieyasu Tokugawa must defeat Katsuie Shibata with a Musou Attack.
# **Okazaki Castle under Threat (Special)** - Prevent Sadatomo Okudaira and Takakiyo Miyake from infiltrating Okazaki Castle.
++++ 2-v2 Battle to Build Sunomata Castle
Playable: (Main only) **Hideyoshi Hashiba** | (Partner only) **Sandayū Momochi**
# **The Battle to Erect Sunomata Castle – 1** - Secure your supplies by escorting the supply captains to Sunomata.
# **The Battle to Erect Sunomata Castle – 2** - Secure your personnel by escorting the engineer captains to Sunomata.
# **Pelting Down** - Defeat Yorimoto Toki and his allies to complete Sunomata Castle.
# **One Thing after Another** - Defeat Michitoshi Nagai and his allies to defend Sunomata Castle.
# **Getting Ahead (Hideyoshi Hashiba Bonus)** – Hideyoshi Hashiba must defeat the enemy troops. (500)
# **The Joy of Castle Construction (Bonus)** – Defeat the enemy troops north of the Oda Main camp to advance the front lines. (100, 2 minutes) [During **The Battle to Erect Sunomata Castle** head North of the main camp to trigger.]
# **Defend It with your Life (Sandayū Momochi Bonus)** - Sandayū Momochi must defeat the enemy troops surrounding Sunomata castle to deploy the shield soldiers. (150, 2 minutes)
# **When No One’s Home (Special)** – Defeat Ittetsu Inaba, Bokzen Ujiie, and Morinari Andō to defend the Oda Main Camp.

++++ 3-^2 Battle of Kannonji Castle
Playable: **Nobunaga Oda**, Mitsuhide Akechi, Hideyoshi Hashiba, Ieyasu Tokugawa, Nagamasa Azai, Nō, Toshiie Maeda, Katsuie Shibata, **Oichi**, Toshimoto Saitō, Shikanosuke Yamanaka, Hanbei Takenaka.

# **The Mysterious Assassin** - Defeat Kazuuji Nakamura to dispel the Oda Army’s confusion.
# **Seizing Wadayama Castle** - Defeat Nagayasu Miyoshi and Jibunotifu Tanaka to capture Wadayama Castle.
# **Elimination** - Defeat all of Kazuuji Nakamura’s doubles to advance toward Kannonji Castle.
# **By Your Side Once More (Nobunaga Oda Bonus)** – Nobunaga Oda must work with Mitsuhide Akechi to defeat the enemy troops.
# **What Must Be Protected (Bonus)** – Defeat the enemy troops to rescue Yoshiaki Ashikaga. [Stay near Yoshiaki, after Kenshin appears and **A Stepping Stone to Kannonji Castle** is cleared wait for him to mention Yoshiaki is the rightful heir to the Shogunate. Oichi must not be a chosen character.]
# **Enemy Attack from the North (Bonus)** – Defeat Sadahime Gamō and Katahide Gamō.
# **Enemy Attack from the Center (Bonus)** – Defeat Masakatsu Miyoshi and Nagafusa Shinohara.
# **A Desperate Defense at Mitsukuri Castle (Bonus)** – Defeat Yoshiharu Rokkaku, Sadatake Hirai, and Sanefusa Ogura to prevent Mitsukuri castle from being recaptured.
# **The Skilled Men of Rokkaku (Bonus)** – Defeat Yoshisada Rokkaku, Sadakage Namazue, and Takaharu Gotō to assist your allies’ advance.
# **For His Protection (Oichi Bonus)** – Oichi must defeat Yoshisada Rokkaku and his allies, and prevent Nagamasa Azai from being taken out.
# **A Show of Skill (Oichi Special)** – Oichi must defeat the enemy troops, and prevent Yoshiaki Ashikaga from being taken out. (100, 3 minutes) [As Oichi have her near Yoshiaki as early as possible.]
++++ 3-^3 Battle of Rokujō

# **Buried Threats (Special)** – Defeat Yotarō Onikojima, and Nagahide Yasuda, and prevent Nobutada Anayama from being taken out.

+ Rare Weapons
This will detail how to get the Rare Weapons of each weapon type, unlike usual games things are done differently here. 
* The difficulty must be Hard or higher.
* You need to get an S rank in all four fields of Time, KO’s, Officer KO’s and Combo Count while also getting the S rank overall reward with the character mentioned, they can be the main character or partner and don’t even need to be used. This can be done in multiple runs and not just one as long as an S was scored at least once in each field.
* It may seem like you have to pick one of the two stages to get a Rare Weapon, but no you have to do both stages with their required characters to get the weapon.
* Whichever difficulty you choose you must get the S rank reward for it throughout your runs if it takes multiple, getting ASSS on Hard then SASS on Nightmare will not count.
* A trick to keep your combo count in stages that have extremely high requirements or very long down times is to switch to your partner as it is fading out to reset the timer when you go back to your character.
