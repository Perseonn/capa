@title: Disgaea 4: A Promise Unforgotten
@author: Buster Wolf
@updated: 2025-04-06

Disgaea 4 is the fourth main game in the strategy RPG series Disgaea. The purpose of this guide is to list item locations for the Main Story, Mt. Ordeal, Post Game and X-Dimension incase you are looking for a particular item for your Data Shop, unless noted any item carried can potentially be of any rarity (Common, Rare or Legendary). If any unique enemies appear on a map they will be listed first followed by the normal enemies that appear, if a map has different enemies from the first time it is done they will be listed separately. Endings will also be listed with a link to a YouTube video incase you want to see the ending again. Chara World Evilities normally learned by all classes are listed incase you are looking for a particular one from the large assortment of evilities the game has.

Note some enemies can't be caught at all which are denoted in-game by a gold S on a green diamond (written as **<S>** in this guide before there name) above their name, this also includes having the DLC character Tyrant Valvatorez with the No Life King Evility, make note of that if a unit you want to capture appears from Item World Enemy Base. Additionally if you do not have the tier of the class you will fail regardless, this includes having Valvatorez at a lower level than the unit you are trying to capture.

If you find any mistakes feel free to notify me (Chara World Evilities of DLC characters would be appreciated).

+ Power Leveling
Feel under powered and need a character to gain levels quickly? Certain maps can be done to gain levels quickly to help boost your characters that need an edge in levels.

**5-2 Lost Dignity**
The map consists of 4 Nekomatas, Hell Hounds, Cockatrices & Nemophilas each in a two by two formation. At Enemy Rank 8 they will all be Level 99 which will give experience given from this is equal to a level 323. The key to leveling is having a unit with at least 8 Movement that can reach the Yellow Geo Block in the lower right corner from the Base Panel and lift it from the lower corner (to not attract a Hell Hound) then throw it onto a Purple panel and destroy the block which covers the field in a EXP +150% effect. The downside to this stage is due to the setup the Economic Stimulus Act bill counts the block as the first kill thus not granting the unit you want to level with the triple EXP benefit.

**9-4 Concrete Knowledge**
The repeated version map consists of 2 lines of 5 Champinions slightly apart from each other. At enemy Rank 1 they will all be Level 99. You may look at the formation and wonder how to finish this map in one attack? Answer Giant Descos Yog Sothoth (learned at Level 100). In terms of leveling you won't gain levels as fast as the stage mentioned above this, but you will finish much faster and with units that have at least 5 Throw you can throw the Geo Blocks onto the Aqua panels straight ahead of it. Also since you are fusing a monster unit with Desco you are essentially leveling 2 units at a time, if your Desco doesn't do enough to kill them in one shot then surround her with allies until she is able to do it by herself with the monster that fused with her. This is a good spot to grind till the stage mentioned below, specially if you have monster units you need their higher class tiers for capturing.

**Mt. Ordeal 4**
The map features 13 Balrogs set in a diamond formation, the key to leveling here is with the use of Giant Magichange. You will need Fusion Weapon Lab and Dual Dojo Evil Symbols unlocked which can give 1 humanoid and up to 4 monsters equal exp per kill. To unlock them you must defeat a level 100 enemy with a giant monster (Fusion Weapon Lab) and defeat a level 1000 enemy with a Magichange (Dual Dojo), you can use a Custom map if needed. If you don't want to make your own custom map you can use Akumajis map (very end of the list by strength ranking) to get your desired Evil Symbol from the Carmilla, Cath Palug & Alraune in the closest row. Note the monster units you want to use for Dual/Giant/Dual Giant Magichange must be in both Evil Symbols not the humanoid units.

Method of leveling based on weapon
**Fist**
Giant Magichange Fist (Cu Sith or Nekomata)
Skill: Big Bang

**Sword**
Giant Magichange Sword (Desco, Orc, Dragon or Succubus) with a Magichanged Rifle Demon (Normal or Giant)
Skills: Rune Dimension + Parallel Cannon
Leave 1 panel free between you and the front most Balrog and use Giant Rune Dimension, then aim Parallel Cannon at the center to hit the two missed.

**Spear**
Giant Magichange Spear (Sludge, Skeletal Dragon or Cockatrice)
Skill: Meteor Shower
Have an ally defeat the center most Balrog then throw your Spear user there and use Giant Meteor Shower.

**Bow**
Giant Magichange Bow (Prinny, Zombie or Death) with another Magichange weapon (for second command)
Skills: Doppelganger + Galaxy Comet
Aim for the center most Balrog with Giant Doppelganger then target the rest with Giant Galaxy Comet.

**Gun**
Giant Magichange Gun (Prinny Kurtis, Mothman, Rifle Demon or Catsaber) with another Magichange weapon (for second command)
Skills: Prism Burster (Secret skill) + Deadly Down Under
Aim Giant Prism Burster at the front most Balrog, then aim Giant Deadly Down Under at the center to hit the four missed.

**Staff**
Giant Magichange Raspberyl with another Magichange weapon (for second command)
Skills: Delta Extreme + Magic Area 7
Stand in front of the front most Balrog and use Giant Delta Extreme, then aim an Area 7 Magic at the center to hit the two missed.

**Universal**
Giant Magichange Raspberyl with a Magichanged Rifle Demon (Normal or Giant)
Skills: Delta Extreme + Parallel Cannon
Stand in front of the front most Balrog and use Giant Delta Extreme, then aim Parallel Cannon at the center to hit the two missed.
