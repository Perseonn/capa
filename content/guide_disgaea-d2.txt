Disgaea D2: A Brighter Darkness is the sequel to [[[guide:disgaea1 | Disgaea 1: Hour of Darkness]]] story wise, this will detail how to view conversations in the game between characters in case you want to go for the **Battle of Words** Trophy (view 100 conversations) or just want to know how the characters interact during battle. For the most part certain characters need to be adjacent to each other outside of the base panel for a conversation to spark denoted by a "!" when they are near each other. Note that conversations only happen the first time you visit a map, new cycles will treat it as a first visit, once the map is cleared conversations will no longer happen.

If you find any conversations I missed feel free to notify me with the level and how you obtained the conversation.

+ Power Leveling
Feel under powered and need a character to gain levels quickly? Certain maps can be done to gain levels quickly to help boost your characters that need an edge in levels. Note that some tips require Post Game abilities to take full advantage such as LoC stat stealing, Evility stealing etc.

**2-2 Cold Breath**
For this stage the Zombie's will not attack you if you are on the fence at the closest you can get to your base panel. The only time the Zombie's will attack you is if they jump on the fence and you didn't kill that one on your turn. The Shaman is the only other threat with the slew of Status Aliments she has at her disposal.

This method will work until Enemy Strength is set to 16, from 17 to 20 the Zombie's will gain Zombie Tornado which bypasses their need to be close to you. 

**2-6 Silver Witch**
Enemy Strength: 7
The Dragon's should be level 33. Kill one Dragon and fuse the other three together making it level 99, the experience given from this is equal to a level 323. You will want to make a tower so that multiple characters will get experience for killing it (they will all get the same amount of experience).

**5-2 Ghostly Daydream**
The only map with an Attack +1 Geo Panel you can take advantage of (outside of hoping for Attack +1 & Invincibility panels in the Item World) for special skill exp as long as the skill doesn't cause the user or target to move. Best done during Post Game on the final reincarnation with maxed Skill EXP (1000%) in the Cheat Shop. The Archers will stay still as long as you aren't in their attack range.

**6-1 Frightening Forest**
Enemy Strength: 9
The Nekomata's should be level 99. They are standing on EXP+50% and Mana+50% panels in two separate groups of two by two. You can if you choose to use a tower for multiple members to gain levels however you will want to be certain the member at the bottom can handle the damage from the Nekomata's as they will rush you on their turns.

**7-3 Overflowing Mirror**
This map is best for increasing Weapon Mastery. The area you want to be in is the Aqua one with Reverse Damage Panels on it. Create up a Martial Artist with the Forced Counter Evility and Double Counter stolen Evility from the Fight Mistress and have them attack the Humanoid you want to increase Weapon Mastery with having Forced Counter as their stolen Evility. Leave the Angel's on the Red Panels alive to take advantage of this as they will never attack you (unless you end turn on the Base Panel) due to the Geo Effect. With the maximum Weapon Mastery setting in the Cheat Shop (1000%) and max Sparring Partner (1900 effective cap) you can go from 0 to 255 in 8 minutes.

**Cave of Ordeal 6**
The repeated version of this map has 2 sets of Rahab's, one in a 2x3 formation with another behind them in a 3x3 formation. All are standing on EXP+100% and Mana+100% panels, a neat thing about them is as long as you stay 7 panels away from them they will not move which are detailed in the diagram below, this only works for magic users to have an easier time leveling up. If you are within 6 panels of them they will attack with Lorenzini which will hit 3 panels in front of their 3 movement range.

++ Maximizing Reincarnation
While the Cheat Shop is able to go up to a maximum of 1000% you may be wondering what is the best setup to get the exact amount of EXP to get to Level 9999 in one battle and enough Mana to reincarnate immediately after. The remaining CP should be put in Weapon Mastery & Skill EXP to keep them from falling too low due to rapid repeated reincarnation, unless it's a monster unit in which all CP should go to Skill EXP.

These tables assume you are using the following
* 3 Emblems made for level grinding
* 1 maxed Guardian (500 effective cap) within the 18 total slots
* 2 maxed Manager's (300 each) within the 18 total slots
* 15 maxed Statistician's (300 each) in the remaining slots

**EXP**
At Level 1 units have a certain amount of EXP needed to get to Level 2 which determines how much total EXP they need to reach Level 9999. You will need 15 full sets of maxed Statistician's (300 per stack) and set the EXP value in the cheat shop to what is listed below. The settings below are based on not having the character in the Punching Bags group in the Demon Dojo, no ally with the Happy Song Evility and LoC Mode set to LoC with at least 1 star at Cave of Ordeal 6.

These tests were done with a Female Angel as they require 18 EXP for Level 2 which means they need the most to get to level 9999.
||~ EXP for Level 2 ||~ EXP at Level 9999 ||~ Cheat Shop EXP setting ||
|| 9 || 45,736,547,562 || 273% ||
|| 12 || 60,982,063,416 || 364% ||
|| 15 || 76,227,579,270 || 455% ||
|| 18 || 91,473,095,124 || 546% ||

**Info dump**
At 1% with the setting above you will get 167,714,490 EXP from finishing CoO6.

For characters who require 9 EXP for Level 2 the lowest in the game they will need the setting at 273% which will give them 45,787,436,985 EXP which is 50,889,423 more EXP than they need to reach Level 9999.

For characters who require 12 EXP for Level 2 the 2nd lowest in the game they will need the setting at 364% which will give them 61,048,075,995 EXP which is 66,012,579 more EXP than they need to reach Level 9999.

For characters who require 15 EXP for Level 2 the 2nd highest in the game they will need the setting at 455% which will give them 76,311,474,990 EXP which is 88,895,720 more EXP than they need to reach Level 9999.

For characters who require 18 EXP for Level 2 the highest in the game they will need the setting at 546% which will give them 91,573,493,985 EXP which is 101,398,861 more EXP than they need to reach Level 9999 (though you'll hit the cap so that extra isn't displayed).

**Mana**
You will need 2 full sets of maxed Manager's (300 per stack) and set the Mana value in the cheat shop to what is listed below. All generic Humanoids and Monsters need the same amount at most with the exception of the Ifrit Class who need more Mana in its Tier 6 class compared to everyone else. The settings below are based on not having the character in the Book Stacks group in the Demon Dojo, no ally with the Fortune Song Evility and LoC Mode set to LoC with at least 1 star at Cave of Ordeal 6.

||~ Character Type ||~ Most mana needed for Reincarnation at Genius ||~ Cheat Shop Mana setting ||
|| Unique || 5,100 || 1% ||
|| Generic Humanoids & Monster || 1,014,499 || 77% ||
|| Ifrit || 1,016,499 || 78% ||

**Info dump**
As Unique characters only need 5,100 Mana putting the setting at 1% will give them 13,185 Mana which is all they will need unless you still need to increase Base Parameters or get more dislikes for Heart's Shadow Boost (if you don't want ally kills for them).

Generic Humanoids and Monsters need at most 999,999 for reincarnation and at most an extra 14,500 Mana to reincarnate at Genius level, with the Mana setting at 77% they will get 1,016,205 Mana.

The Ifrit class is the only class that its Tier 6 class needs 16,500 Mana to reincarnate at Genius level which gives it the total of 1,016,499 which is 294 Mana more than what you would get at 77%, thus if you use an Ifrit the setting should be made 78% so they can get 1,029,405 Mana from one battle.
