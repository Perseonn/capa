Dynasty Warriors 9: Empires is the 5th Empires expansion on the Dynasty warriors side, this guide will go over all forms of stuff you can unlock and conditions for filling the gallery.

+ Historic Event Battles
If certain conditions are met within a certain date there is a chance a historic battle will take place during the scenario meant to mimic the real life event, when triggered you will be given an option to join which will refill your troop count for the battle. These are not unlocked permanently and must be set up if you wish to do them again, with the exception of Wuzhang Plains every battle can be done from their respective scenario.
++ Alliance Against Dong Zhuo
* Date Range: Jan 191-Dec 193
* Dong Zhuo must be a Ruler and control Luoyang while sheltering the Emperor.
* Cao Cao & Yuan Shao must be Rulers.

++ Battle of Guan Du
* Date Range: Apr 200-Dec 202
* Yuan Shao must be a Ruler and control Ye, Guanzong & Beihai.
* Cao Cao must be a ruler and control Puyang, Xuchang & Luoyang.

++ Battle of Chibi
* Date Range: Jun 208-Dec 210
* Cao Cao must be a Ruler and control Xiangyang, Wan & Xuchang.
* Liu Bei must be a Ruler and control Jiangling.
* Sun Quan must be a Ruler and control Jianye, Poyang & Huiji, Zhou Yu must serve in his kingdom.

++ Battle of Wuzhang Plains
* Date Range: Jun 228-Dec 231
* Cao Cao, Cao Pi or Cao Rui must be a Ruler and control Chang'an, Luoyang & Xuchang, Sima Yi must serve in their kingdom.
* Liu Bei or Liu Shan must be a Ruler and control Hanzhong, Chengdu & Tianshui, Zhuge Liang must serve in their kingdom.

+ Titles
Titles are what define your character, based on your titles rarity it will dictate your drops during battle, the difficulty of the scenario also plays a factor in your drop rate which you can see at the bottom right corner when setting your title. The character you control always starts as a Common Officer and you can change your title based on ones you gain throughout your play through, Reputation Bonus is a multiplier to add for your actions to gain experience to help you level certain stats faster or slower.
