Dynasty Warriors 3 is the third mainline Dynasty Warriors game while being the second with the 1 vs 100 formula, unlike [[[guide:dynastywarriors2 | Dynasty Warriors 2]]] there is a lot unlock this time around.

Two words of warning before beginning
* First if you used Cheat Codes with the this game your save cannot be transferred to [[[guide:dynasty-warriors-3-xtreme-legends | Xtreme Legends]]] due to anti cheating measures.
* Second if you have Xtreme Legends and are wondering when should you transfer/mixjoy with the vanilla game do so as early as possible, due to the item Musou Armor requirement rank points will get reset back to 0 if you transfer your save. Your stats will be kept though.
* Third for those playing Complete Edition Remastered the names of battlefields are using the old translation (Hu Lao Gate) not the up to date translation like in-game (Hulao Gate), all of these are in the Original Mode for that version.

+ Character Unlocking/Musou Mode/4th Weapons
This will detail how to unlock all 41 characters for use in Free Mode, while also detailing the Musou Mode for characters of the Shu, Wei & Wu kingdoms and how to get all characters 4th weapon. For 4th weapons the following apply.
* The difficulty must be set to Hard.
* In cases where sub officers are involved if you defeat the main officer, but still manage to defeat the sub officer who is nearby before they retreat it will still meet the requirement.
* If using 2 players as long as one player meets the requirements the weapon will appear.
* The weapon can have either no element or one of the following below.
* Unlike later games only specific attacks with the 4th weapon will have elemental activation.
4th weapons can come with one of the following three elements attached to them
* Fire - Gradually burn the target's life.
* Lightning - Impact with the ground causes a shock wave hitting nearby enemies, can potentially cause another shock wave.
* Steel (Death) - One shot normal peons, deal percentage damage to officers.

++ Item Farming
The best stage to play to get the most items is **The Battle at You Ting - Wu Forces** on Hard. This version gives you 6 item bags to collect compared to Wei's version giving only 4 in different spots. For Wu's version the 6 items bags are.

# Crate at the Southwest corner of the left center path.
# Hu Zhi (East path by Da Qiao)
# Crate South of the center fort
# Man Chong (Southeast of the center fort)
# Cao Xiu
# Crate near Gate Captain behind Sima Yi

If you are wanting to max out all items you will want a general you are good with provided they have Luck on their 4th weapon (Gan Ning is a good candidate for quick clearing) with Seven Star Orb as one of their items. Quit and save **before** you start picking up items as bags picked up before saving will already have the contents determined when you beat the stage. If you don't get an upgrade to your items press Start+Select, reload the save and try again, this will require patience specially for Wind Scroll & Elixir as they are pretty rare.
