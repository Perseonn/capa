Disgaea 2: Cursed Memories is the second mainline game in the strategy RPG series Disgaea. The purpose of this guide is to list item locations for Main Story, Dark World, Bonus Worlds and Axel Mode in case you are looking for a particular item type to steal with a Thief for those playing Dark Hero Days (PSP) or PC (Steam) for completion of the Collection Record at the Record Shop. Unless noted any item they carry can potentially be of any rarity (Common, Rare or Legendary). If any unique enemies appear on a map they will be listed first followed by the normal enemies that appear, if a map has a different enemy from the first time it is done they will be listed separately. Endings will also be listed with a link to a YouTube video incase you want to see the ending again.

+ Power Leveling
Feel under powered and need a character to gain levels quickly? Certain maps can be done to gain levels quickly to help boost your characters that need an edge in levels. Unfortunately there aren't many dedicated maps to level grind progressively through the story, but these few will still be helpful when you reach them.

**3-3 Corpse Garden (Adell Dark World)**
This stage is mostly for farming via the bonus gauge using Beast Masters with Bows in safe spots while you have a Thief or Kunoichi Sleep and Poison the Zombie to finish the map.

**9-2 Grave of Alchemy**
The map is split between two groups of 2 Magic Knights & 2 Star Skull/Mages standing on EXP +50% panels, slowly passing 8 Strong Enemy bills (or 1 Even Stronger & 3 Strong for later ports) will make the Magic Knights be at level 99 which gives as much experience as a level 323 enemy when defeated, any higher and you will not gain as much.

**3-4 Dissection Show (Axel Dark World)**
This is best done during your trip into Axel Mode even from a new game using the code to start here, you will need a Thief at least at level 20 for Consciousness (Sleep) and Freedom (Paralysis) to be learned and 3 throwers with 3 Shoes equipped on them. Have your Thief Sleep the Archer and bring out 2 throwers and have one lift and throw the unit 3 tiles to the left with a glitch similar to diagonal throwing. The unit you threw start sending them up to the Death Blow Geo Symbol and end turn unless they can make it without ending turn. Get another unit to throw and after they make it through throw the first one closer to the Death Blow then lift and throw it on the Blue panel, have your Thief use Freedom to Paralyze the Archer and have your desired unit strike her to level up. Her original level is 2000, but if you pass the Strongest Enemy Bill she will be level 9999, the amount of EXP she'll give at 9999 is 12,199,642 without any felony exp modifier which is roughly 6 times more than normal Ordeal 4. This is only better if you aren't able to clear Land of Carnage Ordeal 4 yet as it is much faster.

**Ordeal 4**
This will basically be your home (specially on PS2) until you get the Land of Carnage version, this map is filled with Mad Jesters in a 2x4 formation on EXP+100 panels. Due to the formation this map will always take 2 attacks unless you have Zetta upgraded by Mao to Magichange 2 to use his Zetta Beeeam to level more at once.

+ Axel Mode
A side story meant to take place before Adell's story, to access Adell mode you must finish from an ending after beating Zenon to be given the choice to do Axel mode. This will give you access to Magichange for Adell mode whenever you go through this short story. To play this first however on the main menu highlight New Game and press Triangle, Square, Circle, Triangle, Square, Circle, X. If you hear Axel's voice that should confirm that it worked.
