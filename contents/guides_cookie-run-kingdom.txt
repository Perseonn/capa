+ [[# first-thing]]I'm new / I just returned after months, what cookies should I get?
* Most Story Content, at least to Crispia 18, can be cleared with 3 cookies: [[span class="icon cine"]]Cine (Fettuccine)[[/span]], [[span class="icon creme-brulee"]]Creme Brulee[[/span]], and [[span class="icon star-coral"]]Star Coral[[/span]].
 * The other 2 cookies are flexible.
 * Raise only 5 cookies to max level
 * Arena is out of reach for you team as you don't have max leveled cookies, you should use your Story mode team for Arena
* Treasures are important; use [[span class="icon trsr scroll"]]Scroll[[/span]], [[span class="icon trsr watch"]]Watch[[/span]], [[span class="icon trsr whistle"]]Whistle[[/span]].
 * If you have [[span class="icon creme-brulee"]]Creme Brulee[[/span]] in party, use [[span class="icon trsr robes"]]Robe[[/span]] instead
 * Use [[span class="icon trsr rare-scroll"]]Rare Scroll[[/span]], and [[span class="icon trsr common-watch"]]Common Watch[[/span]] if these treasure's levels are higher.

+ [[# toppings]] What Toppings?
* In general, Cookies's topping can be grouped by:
 * [[span class="icon top almond"]]Almonds[[/span]]: Defense
 * [[span class="icon top choco"]]Chocolates[[/span]]: Support, Healer
 * [[span class="icon top rasp"]]Raspberries[[/span]]: Everyone else
* **Exceptions exist!** Guild Battle, Arena and certain tough bosses will require more specific setups.

++ Topping Grinding Strategy
* Find Epic (M-sized) toppings (priority on [[span class="icon top almond"]]Almonds[[/span]] and [[span class="icon top choco"]]Chocolates[[/span]]) from Story mode.
* Upgrade Epic (M-sized) topping to +6 and check the substat you get.
* [[span class="icon top rasp"]]Raspberries[[/span]] ideally have at least 4% damage resist substat, but you can ignore this if you don't have the resource.
* [[span class="icon top almond"]]Almonds[[/span]] should have at least 4% damage resist substat.
* [[span class="icon top choco"]]Chocolates[[/span]] should have at least 1% cooldown substat
* Once you get the good substat, grind the topping to +12.
* If your first substat is undesired like Amplify Buff, Critical Resist, Debuff Resist, break the topping down for a little refund. Others like HP, Attack, or Def might still be useful for Alliance, or stock toppings should the need arise.

+ [[# beascuits]] I keep failing attuning Beascuits!
* Always Attune with moderation.
* If after 4 attunes, the beascuit doesn't have 2 slots, break it.
* If after 8 attunes, the beascuit doesn't have 3 slots, break it.
* If after 12 attunes, the beascuit doesn't have 4 slots, break it.


+ [[# mota]] Who should I get from Might of the Ancients?
* Get one you currently don't have, or wait until you get most of them and then pick one you don't have.
* Newbies usually have much higher luck, so it won't be long until you have all 5 ancients.


+ [[# best-legendary]] Who is the best Legendary?
* Assuming you are asking about the Legendary Cookie Choice Chest.
* It's always good to pick the newest Legendary from the box (usually the leftmost one).
* You also can keep the box and see which Legendary becomes much more useful for Guild Battle.
 * Example, as of writing, Frost Queen is used against Living Abyss, and Wind Archer is used on Arena.

+ [[# coins]] Where do I get coins?
Tree of Wishes, there's no better way than this, but take these notes:
* On weekends, there's Burning Time event that increases the coins by 20%; this is the best time to go overboard with the Tree
* Unless you are swimming in materials, never give out your Smithy items, except axes and pickaxes
* Unless featured in Golden Wish or give you a generous amount (50k+ coins), don't give out lower tier materials, e.g. don't give cotton candy when you have access to Pom Jelly, and don't give Pom Jelly if you have the Colourful Bowl.
* Refresh wishes if you don't have enough materials.
* Almost every aspect in cookie development requires coins. You gain nothing by skipping the tree!
* Please do not use the Coin Bounties as this will create a bad dependency.
 * Bounties should be used only for Powders, Sugar Crystals, and Cake Pieces.

++ [[# exp]] How do I get more EXP?
* Cookie Mansion is THE best way to obtain EXP, as well as the most tedious
* At Castle L12, with 18 Cookie Mansion L10, you get 560714 EXP per day
* At Castle L13, with 20 Cookie Mansion L11, you get 653240 EXP per day
* Special Episodes gives 400 EXP per Quill used, or 14400 EXP per day without ads
* Each fully upgraded landmark from kingdom pass gives 9600 EXP per day
* Bounties can give over 100K EXP on a 100% boost day per Level 15 run
 * Do note that bounties are not a recommended way to get EXP, due to the much more valuable Skill Powders, Sugar Crystals, and Cake Pieces.

++ [[# treasure]] Which treasure should I get?
* Convert your Mileage Point to Treasure Tickets, focusing on:
 * [[span class="icon trsr scroll"]]Old Pilgrim Scroll[[/span]]
 * [[span class="icon trsr whistle"]]Dream Conductor's Whistle[[/span]]
 * [[span class="icon trsr robes"]]Librarian's Enchanted Robes[[/span]]
* While waiting there to be maxed, you can use the [[span class="icon trsr rare-scroll"]]Disciple's Magic Scroll (Rare)[[/span]], [[span class="icon trsr common-watch"]]Squishy Jelly Watch (Common)[[/span]], and [[span class="icon trsr scythe"]]Grim Looking Scythe (Rare)[[/span]].
* [[span class="icon trsr watch"]] Sleepyhead's Jelly Watch[[/span]] at Level 12 only adds 5% Attack over the [[span class="icon trsr common-watch"]]Squishy Jelly Watch (Common)[[/span]], so you can put it on lower priority since Beascuits can easily fill that 5% Attack earlier.

++ [[# landmark]] Which landmark is the best?
* Stat-based landmarks needs to be maxed as soon as possible
 * Dreaming Lion Statue (Atk) from Sculpture Park
 * Dark Lord Castle (Damage Resist) from Crimson Badlands
 * Eerie Haunted House (Crit%) from Withces & Ghosts
 * Everwinter Snow Globe (Def) from Kingdom of Snow & Ice
 * Chocolate Altar of the Fallen (Crit DMG) from Kingdom of the Unwavering
 * Morning Dew Garden (HP) from Flower Garden
 * Ominous Cake Tower (HP) from St. Pastry Order
 * Eternal Hero Memorial (HP for Ancients) from Might of the Ancients
* Supporting landmarks that helps Stamina Jelly regenerations will help you in the long run with progress, EXP, Soulstones, and Soulcores
 * Tick Tock Clock Tower (Production Speed) from Crispy Cookie Town
 * Grand Dessert Tower (Stamina Jellies) from Garden of Delights
 * Skull Island Resort (Stamina Jellies) from Tropical Island
 * Ferris Wheel (Random Soulstones) from Bustling Amusement Park
 * Gumball Arcade Machine (Random Soulstones) from Pew-Pew Arcade

++ [[# research]] Which Research should I do?
* Prioritize All Cookies ATK Bonus, All Cookies DEF Bonus, and All Cookies HP Bonus. These should be Level 10
* After that, you can go for per-type bonus, starting from ATK Bonus I and II, DEF Bonus I and II, then HP Bonus I and 2. These should be at least Level 5.

++ [[# statue]] What is the statue of Statue of Radiance?
* Statue of Radiance is an upgradable thing that resides in Hall of Ancient Heroes.
* You need Radiant Shards to upgrade the statue.
* You gain small stats per level, but these stats stacks quickly.
* Try to have L50 statue as soon as possible, you can slow down after, but never forget to gather Radiant Shards.

++ [[# bonds]] What are Bonds?
* Check your bonds by going to the Cookies screen and tap on the bottom-left "Bonds" button! 
* Getting certain cookies to @<&#11088;>@5 will maximize your bond level and in turn, increase your stats.
* Madelaine cookie is notable for having 3 bonds at once, 2 of which is with rare and common cookies.

++ [[# relics]] What are Guild Relics?
* Guild relics are Guild properties that gives certain stat boost to each member.
* Typically you will want to join Guild with high level relic.
 * However, you need to be strong in the first place to be able to join one.
 * Thus you might want to start by joining casual guild who can reach **at least Master 1** in Guild Battle.
  * A Grandmaster 3 guild is more preferable.
* Gathering Guild relic is cumbersome, you cannot do it with just 1-2 friends, especially since people will look for guild with complete relic, so better just join an existing guild. 

+ [[# resonant]] What's a Mysterious Toppings or Resonant Toppings?
* A Mysterious Toppings contains several Resonant Toppings
* Resonant Toppings are Epic Toppings with better minimum substat amount and less useless substats. It only has attack, attack speed, critical%, cooldown, damage resist, and critical resist as substats.
* Resonant Toppings can only be equipped by certain cookies.
