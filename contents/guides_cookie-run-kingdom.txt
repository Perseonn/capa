@author: Perseonn Balthasaar

This guide is intended as a tips list for people who are overwhelmed by the game, as both a newbie and a returnee. If you are confused on what to do with the game, read on! Note that long-term progression and resource efficiency will be prioritized here over immediately maximizing your combat power. Several things will get repeated as each section was written as a stand alone.

[[include comp:alert-warning
|text=Cookie Run Kingdom requires massive amount of time sink. If you're playing to collect cookies, it's fine. If you want to experience the entire game, it's time-consuming. Cookie Castle L15 is 8 months, opening lands are 1.5 years, lab researches are 2 years, guild building is over 3 years and so on.

If you want to be successful in the game, you'll need to pay attention to **resource management**.
]]

[[#important]]
+ The Most Important Thing
* Go to the Option Menu
* Go to the Notifications tab
* Turn off Global Messages
* No more random people getting cookies messages!

[[image crk-global-notification.webp | For your piece of mind, turn this option off!]]

+ What to Do?
Check the quest menu on the bottom of the Kingdom screen.

++ Daily Routines in Kingdom
* Never stop sending balloons
 * Balloon is free to send, and it brings [[i|crk radiant-shard]] Radiant Shards, [[i|crk topping-piece]] Topping Pieces (Crispia), [[i|crk beascuit-dough]] Beascuit Dough (Beast Yeast)
* Never stop sending trains
 * Trains are your main income for [[i|crk aurora-pillar]] Aurora Mats. No Aurora mats = No Kingdom growth.
 * Aurora mats acquisition are badly randomized. Lopsided distribution among the [[i|crk aurora-soap]] Bricks, [[i|crk aurora-compass]]Compasses, and [[i|crk aurora-pillar]] Pillars are a common occurrence.
* Never stop sending ships on Touc's Trade Harbor
 * Ships bring [[i|crk rainbow-pearl]] Rainbow Pearls, which can be used to good stuff in Rainbow Shell Gallery
* Do your Tree of Wishes daily to obtain all 3 Daily Rewards.

+++ On Sending Balloon to Crispia vs Beast Yeast
* Sending Balloon to Crispia Yields [[i|crk topping-piece]] Topping Pieces (Crispia).
 * [[i|crk topping-piece]] Topping pieces are well used all the way to late game. Amassing them is important.
* Sending Balloon to Beast Yeast [[i|crk beascuit-dough]] Beascuit Dough (Beast Yeast) + Yeast Ore
 * The most important Yeast Ore is [[i|crk yeast-ore-deceit]] Deceit Yeast Ore from Beast Yeast Episode 7 and 8, which increases [[i|crk rainbow-pearl]] Rainbow Pearl yield from Ships on Touc's Trade Harbor.

[[include comp:alert-warning
|text=Fully collecting 60 Yeast Ores takes 200 days if you use all 3 Beast Yeast Expedition Attempts per day.
]]

++ Bounties
* Refill your bounties daily
* [[i|crk skill-powder]] Skill Powders are of the highest priority since this is its primary source.
* [[i|crk sc-all]]Sugar Crystals can be considered on days were sugar crystal is boosted, but always prioritize Skill Powders when both are boosted. There are other ways to get [[i|crk sc-all]] Sugar Crystals like Master Mode, or Guild Gacha.
* [[i|crk cake-piece]] Chaos Cake Pieces can be considered, but I personally think there's no need to get it from bounties as it is never boosted, and can be obtained from Sublime Cake Tower and Rainbow Shell Gallery.
* EXP and Coins are absolute no-no. Doing this will create bad dependency!

++ Mine
* Prioritize mining [[i|crk fossil-amber]] Amber Fossil on early game.
 * It will help with getting [[i|crk butter-amber]] Butter Amber for Topping Tarts, [[i|crk attunement-kits]] Attunement Kits, and [[i|crk radiant-shard]] Radiant Shards.
* Dedicate at least 1 Mine slot to [[i|crk fossil-emerald]].
* Never mine for Purple Fossil. You can get beascuits from other sources.
* Melt agar cubes as often as you can
* [[i|crk sugar-ore]] Sugar ore acquisition is quite slow. Only use it to make Epic Topping Tarts, and never use it to make Rare Topping Tarts.

++ Arena
* Do your best at arena to burn all the tickets you have.
* Don't let defeat deter you. Your main aim is to deplete the ticket stock to gain [[i|crk arena-medal]] Arena Medals.

+++ Using Weak Defense to Farm Medals
* Once you can afford to have Arena meta cookies, you can start using weak Defense to farm [[i|crk arena-medal]] Arena Medals.
* By using weak defense, you're more likely to encounter weaker teams on your defense record than stronger ones.
* You don't need to attack anyone, just wait.
* From my experience, I have ~80% win rate over 1200+ battles each season. Since 1 victory averages at 20 medals, I get ~20 000 medals per season.

++ Nether Exploration
* Nether Exploration has Healing -65%, so it's a good idea to use standard template comp that consist of: 1 cookie that can tank / take damage, 2 cookies that can do damage, and 2 cookies that heals.
* Elemental Tanks are rare, so feel free to use Charge Cookies to replace them, such as [[i|crk pyam]][[i|crk mc-pyam]] Purple Yam for Grass team.
* Healing and Support cookies doesn't need to match element. It's good if they can Elemental Enchant like [[i|crk bartender]][[i|crk mc-bartender]] Sparkling, but not a requirement.
* If you see [[i|crk mcgonagall]][[i|crk mc-mcgonagall]] Blue Berry Pie on the roster list, be prepared to get stumped with the electric team on your 1st run.
* Most important thing is: do your best.
 * Get as much boss nodes as you can on Floor 1-5.
 * The Hard modem particularly Floor 6+ is aimed for established players on an established guilds.
 * If you feel your 1st run isn't good, think twice before redoing it, as a run can take 40 minutes!


+ Should I Make a Guild?
Join a guild, never make one.

The amount of resource and time commitment you need to build a guild from scratch is enormous. You better go look for a very casual [[i|crk rank-gm3]]Grandmaster 3 (GM3) or higher guild. A Grandmaster guild is easily distinguishable by visiting the guild and check the golden banner near the Beacon.

+ Which Cookies Should I Get?
[[#first-thing]]
++ General Rule of Team Comp
* A common team comp is: 1 cookie that can tank / take damage, 2 cookies that can do damage, and 2 cookies that heals.
 * Most [[i|crk cl-defense]] Defense cookie can take damage, but not all of them tanks or redirect damage taken by allies.
  * [[i|crk legolas-papa]] Millennial Tree is known for being both a tank and a healer, despite being a [[i|crk cl-support]] Support Cookie
 * Most [[i|crk cl-healing]] Healing and [[i|crk cl-support]] Support cookies can heal, but few Support cookies cannot heal.
* This comp is a template for all high difficulty content, such as Hard Mode and Nether Exploration.

++ For Exploration Story Mode
* Most Story Content, at least to Crispia 18, can be cleared with 3 cookies: [[i|crk cine]] Cine (Fettuccine), [[i|crk creme-brulee]] Creme Brulee, and [[i|crk lantern-girl]] Star Coral.
 * If you have an unopened Legendary Box, get [[i|crk legolas]] Wind Archer instead of [[i|crk creme-brulee]] Creme Brulee.
 * The other 2 cookies are flexible.
 * Raise only 5 cookies to max level
 * Use your Story mode team for Arena, and just do your best spending the tickets.
* Treasures are important; use [[i|crk trsr scroll]] Scroll, [[i|crk trsr watch]] Watch, [[i|crk trsr whistle]] Whistle.
 * If you have [[i|crk creme-brulee]] Creme Brulee in party, use [[i|crk trsr robes]] Robe instead
 * Use [[i|crk trsr rare-scroll]] Rare Scroll, and [[i|crk trsr common-watch]] Common Watch if these treasure's levels are higher.

[[#mota]]
++ Who Should I Get from Might of the Ancients / Beast?
* Get one you currently don't have, or wait until you get most of them and then pick one you don't have.
 * Ancient: Consider [[i|crk white-lily]] White Lily, [[i|crk hollyberry]]Hollyberry, then anyone you don't currently have.
 * Beast: [[i|crk kuso-piero]] Shadow Milk, [[i|crk mamiko-noto]] Eternal Sugar, [[i|crk kikuko-inoue]] Mystic Flour, then anyone you don't currently have.

[[#best-legendary]]
++ Who to Pick from the Legendary Box?
[[i|crk legolas]] Wind Archer. If you already have Wind Archer, wait until you get either [[i|crk legolas-papa]] Millennial Tree or [[i|crk sugar-swan]] Sugar Swan, then pick the one you don't have from the box.

++ Who to Pick from the Pavilion of Promise?
* Get one of each.
* After that, focus on [[i|crk squirrel]] Cream Ferret and [[i|crk bunny]] Marshmallow Bunny.

[[#toppings]]
+ What Toppings Should I Use?
* In general, Cookies' toppings can be grouped by:
 * [[i|crk top almond]] Almonds: Defense
 * [[i|crk top choco]] Chocolates: Support, Healer
 * [[i|crk top rasp]] Raspberries: Everyone else
* **Exceptions exist!** Guild Battle, Arena and certain tough bosses will require more specific setups.

++ Where Do I Get Topping Pieces?
* [[i|crk topping-piece]] Topping Pieces can be obtained from sending Balloon to Crispia.
* [[i|crk topping-piece]] Topping Pieces can be obtained from Guild Gacha.
* [[i|crk topping-piece]] Topping Pieces can be obtained from breaking down Rare and Common Toppings.
* [[i|crk topping-piece]] Topping Pieces can be obtained from breaking down Epic Toppings with undesirable substat to regain some of the used [[i|crk topping-piece]] Topping Pieces

++ Topping Grinding Strategy
* Find Epic (M-sized) toppings (priority on [[i|crk top almond]] Almonds and [[i|crk top choco]] Chocolates) from Story mode.
* Upgrade Epic (M-sized) topping to +6 and check the substat you get.
* [[i|crk top rasp]] Raspberries ideally have at least 4% damage resist substat, but you can ignore this if you don't have the resource.
* [[i|crk top almond]] Almonds should have at least 4% damage resist substat.
* [[i|crk top choco]] Chocolates should have at least 1% cooldown substat
* Once you get the good substat, grind the topping to +12.
* If your first substat is undesired like Amplify Buff, Critical Resist, Debuff Resist, break the topping down for a little refund. Others like HP, Attack, or Def might still be useful for Alliance, or stock toppings should the need arise.

+++ Higher Standard for Topping Grinding Strategy
* ⚠️ Only applies if you have enough toppings for your meta cookies
* For all toppings: at least 2.5% out of 3%; 1.7% out of 2%; 5% out of 6% for substat. Some exceptions apply if the substat is a golden combination, such as Damage Resist + Attack + Cooldown or something similar.
* Caramels need to have Attack Speed on +6, else broken.
* Almonds need to have Damage Resist on +6, else broken.
* Peanuts need to have HP on +6, else broken.
* Chocos need to have Cooldown on +6, else broken.
* Same with Walnut, Kiwi, Candy, and Hazelnut, but only grind this if you have resources to spare.

++ Topping Tarts
* Mine [[i|crk fossil-amber]] Amber Fossil to obtain [[i|crk butter-amber]] Butter Amber.
* You can directly mine [[i|crk butter-amber]] Butter Amber in the early game to accelerate your Topping Tart collection.
* Any Epic or Legendary Topping Tarts without at least green stat gets broken down.
* Walnut, Kiwi, Candy, and Hazelnut need to have purple stat, else broken down.
* Peanut, Caramel, and Almond need to have blue stat, else broken down.

[[#resonant]]
++ What's a Mysterious Topping or Resonant Topping?
* A Mysterious Topping contains several Resonant Toppings.
* Resonant Toppings are Epic Toppings with better minimum substat amount and less useless substats. It only has attack, attack speed, critical%, cooldown, damage resist, and critical resist as substats.
* Resonant Toppings can only be equipped by certain cookies.



[[#beascuits]]
+ Where Do I Get Beascuits?
* Daily Monster Menace, regardless of chest.
* Beast Yeast cost is discounted on weekends, so I usually hunt for Support or other required elemental Beascuits on weekends.

++ Where Do I Get Radiant Beascuit Doughs?
* Spend Fate Jellies for [[i|crk radiant-dough]] Radiant Beascuit Dough.
* Break down unused Beascuits for [[i|crk radiant-dough]] Radiant Beascuit Dough.
* Look for Bell Buff from Town Square that reduces Reset and Single Reset Cost by 20% before tuning beascuits.

++ Attuning Beascuits Strategy
* Always Attune with moderation.
* If after 4 attunes, the beascuit doesn't have 2 slots, break it.
* If after 8 attunes, the beascuit doesn't have 3 slots, break it.
* If after 12 attunes, the beascuit doesn't have 4 slots, break it.
* You can use [[i|crk attunement-kits]] Attunement Kits if you want, but keep the above breakpoints.
* [[i|crk fossil-amber]]Amber Fossil can give you [[i|crk attunement-kits]] Attunement Kits.



[[#coins]]
+ Where Do I Get Coins?
Tree of Wishes, there's no better way than this, but take these notes:
* On weekends, there's Burning Time event that increases the coins by 20%; this is the best time to go overboard with the Tree.
* A couple of points on early game Tree:
 * Unless you are swimming in materials, never give out your Smithy items, except axes and pickaxes.
 * Unless featured in Golden Wish or give you a generous amount (50k+) of coins, don't give out lower tier materials, e.g. don't give cotton candy when you have access to Pom Jelly, and don't give Pom Jelly if you have the Fluffy Castella.
* Refresh wishes if you don't have enough materials.
* Almost every aspect in cookie development requires coins. You gain nothing by skipping the tree!
* Unless it's the only item left, do not purchase Coins from Event Shops.
* Please do not use the Coin Bounties as this will create a bad dependency.
 * Bounties should be used only for [[i|crk skill-powder]] Powders, [[i|crk sc-all]]Sugar Crystals, and [[i|crk cake-piece]] Chaos Cake Pieces.

[[image crk-tree-of-wishes.webp | Tree of Wishes is your main source of coins. Please don't try to find alternatives.]]

[[#get-stronger]]
+ How Do I Get Stronger?
You can get stronger by maximizing these things:
* Cookie Level
* Cookie Skill Level
* Treasures
* Toppings
* Beascuit
* Landmarks
* Research
* Statue of Radiance
* Bonds
* Costume Sets
* Guild Relic

[[#exp]]
++ How Do I Get More EXP?
* Cookie Mansion is THE best way to obtain EXP, as well as the most tedious.
 * Each house level is minor increase, but the multiplier is too big to ignore, since it's = {{[House EXP] * [Number of Houses] * [Days you are Playing]}}
* Go on Master Mode, once you are powerful enough, to convert stamina Jellies to EXP, Colored Sugar Crystal, and [[i|crk lse]] Legendary Soul Essence
* Bounties are not a recommended way to get EXP, due to the much more valuable Skill Powders, Sugar Crystals, and Cake Pieces.

++ How Do I Get Skill Powders and Sugar Crystals?
* Bounties. Refill your bounties daily.
* Your early game bounties should be strictly [[i|crk skill-powder]] Skill Powders and [[i|crk sc-all]]Sugar Crystals. 
* [[i|crk sc-all]]Sugar Crystals can also be obtained from Master Mode and Guild Gacha.
* Late game where you have steady income of Sugar Crystals, your bounties should be strictly Skill Powders.

[[#treasure]]
++ Which Treasure Should I Get?
* Convert your Mileage Point to Treasure Tickets, focusing on:
 * [[i|crk trsr scroll]] Old Pilgrim Scroll
 * [[i|crk trsr whistle]] Dream Conductor's Whistle
 * [[i|crk trsr robes]] Librarian's Enchanted Robes
* Don't use your Crystal on Treasure Gacha!
* While waiting there to be maxed, you can use the [[i|crk trsr rare-scroll]] Disciple's Magic Scroll (Rare), [[i|crk trsr common-watch]] Squishy Jelly Watch (Common), and [[i|crk trsr scythe]] Grim Looking Scythe (Rare).
* [[i|crk trsr watch]]  Sleepyhead's Jelly Watch at Level 12 only adds 5% Attack over the [[i|crk trsr common-watch]] Squishy Jelly Watch (Common), so you can put it on lower priority since Beascuits can easily fill that 5% Attack earlier.

[[#landmark]]
++ Which Landmarks Should I Get?
* Stat-based landmarks needs to be maxed as soon as possible.
 * Dreaming Lion Statue (Atk) from Sculpture Park.
 * Dark Lord Castle (Damage Resist) from Crimson Badlands.
 * Eerie Haunted House (Crit%) from Withces & Ghosts.
 * Everwinter Snow Globe (Def) from Kingdom of Snow & Ice.
 * Chocolate Altar of the Fallen (Crit DMG) from Kingdom of the Unwavering.
 * Morning Dew Garden (HP) from Flower Garden.
 * Ominous Cake Tower (HP) from St. Pastry Order.
 * (Optional) Eternal Hero Memorial (HP for Ancients) from Might of the Ancients.
* Supporting landmarks that helps Stamina Jelly regenerations will help you in the long run with progress, EXP, Soulstones, and Soulcores.
 * Tick Tock Clock Tower (Production Speed) from Crispy Cookie Town.
 * Grand Dessert Tower (Stamina Jellies) from Garden of Delights.
 * Skull Island Resort (Stamina Jellies) from Tropical Island.
 * Ferris Wheel (Random Soulstones) from Bustling Amusement Park.
 * Gumball Arcade Machine (Random Soulstones) from Pew-Pew Arcade.

[[#research]]
++ Which Research Should I Do?
In order of priority:
* Cookies Tab: Tastier Star Jellies.
* Kingdom Tab: Research Efficiency I and Research Efficiency II.
* Cookies Tab: All Cookies ATK Bonus, All Cookies DEF Bonus, and All Cookies HP Bonus. These should be Level 10.
* Cookies Tab: Per-type bonus, starting from ATK Bonus I and II, DEF Bonus I and II, then HP Bonus I and II. Everything should be at least Level 5, before going for Level 6+.

[[#statue]]
++ What is the Statue of Radiance?
* Statue of Radiance is an upgradable thing that resides in Hall of Ancient Heroes.
* Statue of Radiance increases your House EXP and Coins from Trees, as well as raises Cookies' Attack, Defense and HP.
* You need [[i|crk radiant-shard]] Radiant Shards to upgrade the statue.
* You gain small stats per level, but the stats stacks quickly.
* Try to have L50 statue as soon as possible, you can slow down after, but always remember to gather [[i|crk radiant-shard]] Radiant Shards.

+++ Where Do I Get Radiant Shards?
* Balloon is your main [[i|crk radiant-shard]] Radiant Shards income.
* [[i|crk mm-chest-item]] Monster Menace Item Chest.
* Arena Shop and Guild Battle Shop if you have the currencies.

[[#bonds]]
++ What are Bonds?
* Check your bonds by going to the Cookies screen and tap on the bottom-left "Bonds" button! 
* Getting certain cookies to ⭐5 will maximize your bond level and in turn, increase your stats.
* [[i|crk mado-chan]] Madelaine cookie is notable for having 3 bonds at once, 2 of which is with rare and common cookies.

[[#relics]]
++ What are Guild Relics?
* Guild relics are Guild properties that gives certain stat boost to each member.
* Typically you will want to join Guild with high level relic.
 * However, you need to be strong in the first place to be able to join one.
 * Thus you might want to start by joining casual guild who can reach **at least** [[i|crk rank-m1]] **Master 1**  in Guild Battle.
  * [[i|crk rank-gm3]] Grandmaster 3 or higher guild is more preferable.
* Gathering Guild relic is cumbersome, it's practically impossible to do it with just 1-2 friends, especially since people will look for guild with complete relic, so better just join an existing guild. 

++ Where Do I Get Legendary Soul Essences?
* [[i|crk lse]] Legendary Soul Essence is a Master Mode drop, but typically only 1-2 per day, assuming you use 400 Stamina Jellies.
* [[i|crk lse]] Legendary Soul Essence is frequent on Event Shops.
* [[i|crk lse]] Legendary Soul Essence is a byproduct if you get extra soulstone from maxed Super Epic+ Cookies
 * Always get [[i|crk mm-chest-soul]] Monster Menace Soulstone Chest
 * Always exchange Radiant Ore Vein Crystals for Mine Battle cards and melt all agar cubes. Weekends cuts their melting time by 50%, so you can use fewer Express Tickets.
 * If you are swimming on gems: wait for Legendary aggregate cookie banner and spend Gems on it.
