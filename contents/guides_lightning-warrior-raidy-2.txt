Lightning Warrior Raidy II is the sequel to [http://capa.wikidot.com/guide:lightningwarriorraidy2 the first game].

Very few changes have been made from the first game notably there are no maps to collect, now as you walk through the area your mini map will fill in parts you have stepped on. Raidy can now have a Sword and Shield, 2 Swords or 2 Shields as her weapon choices, some swords are two handed though. Raidys Thunder Slash has been tweaked to now charge up to increase its damage in battle at the cost of 10 MP per charge, the damage being calculated as **Level x 3 per charge** for damage when unleashed. For example charging three times at level 10 then unleashing a Thunder Slash will deal 90 damage (10x3x3). Gold has been added to buy items from the shops though enemies will not always drop them.

This guide will be going for all scenes & graphics, I will attempt to minimize backtracking as much as possible since it is very easy to over level if you are going for all of them. The material in this game is not meant players who are underage. The amount of EXP monsters will give is **EXP - Raidys level** for example Mercenary will give you 20 EXP when you beat one at Level 1 (21-1) so you will get less EXP as you level up. Suggested level is mainly for a balanced playthrough not too high and not too low for safety or if you want an easier time feel free to be one level higher than the suggested level.

+ Stats
**HP:** Your health. If it runs out, it's Game Over.
Whenever you gain a level, max HP increases by a random number ranging from 10 to 21.

**MP:** Your mana. Used to charge up and deliver a powerful fixed damage attack, can charge up to 18 times.
Whenever you gain a level, max MP increases by a random number ranging from 10 to 21.

**AP R/L:** Your accuracy stat for Raidys hand with a weapon equipped, her Left hand will always have 4 less AP than her right. It determines how often your attacks actually hit.
Whenever you gain a level, AP increases by 1 point.

**DMG R/L:** Your damage stat for Raidys hand with a weapon equipped. It determines the amount of damage your attacks do.
Whenever you gain a level, DMG increases by 4 points.

**DEF:** Your defense stat. It determines the amount of damage you receive from enemy normal attacks.
Whenever you gain a level, DEF increases by 3 points.

**PP:** Your evasion stat. It determines how often you evade enemy normal attacks.
Whenever you gain a level, PP increases by 1 point.

+ Temple Floor 3
Suggested Level: 35
Final area of the game (also the smallest), if you didn't hit the level cap (35) you are sure to hit it here. To fight Gav you will need to get two statues from doors with a soul in order to open his room.

**Final Boss**
Priest Gav
HP: 750

His normal attack will hit for around 45 damage while his Curse does 96 damage (64 with Magical Bandanna). Use a Heavy Heal Potion at around 100 HP. After defeating him he will transform and begin the second phase of the battle.

Gav
HP: 1300

His normal attack will hit for around 68 damage while his Bless does 105 damage (70 with Magical Bandanna). Use a Heavy Heal Potion at around 150 HP.

Regardless which phase of Gav you lose to you will get the same lose scene. Enjoy the ending.
