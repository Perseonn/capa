If you meant to look for [[[guide:dynastywarriors | Dynasty Warriors]]] the fighting game this is the wrong guide. Dynasty Warriors (PSP) is KOEI's first game that was made for the Playstation Portable, unlike Dynasty Warriors 4 which most of the assets are pulled from you go through a grid based map in attempts to eventually make it to the enemy main camp and take it over. Battles always start you at Lv.1 with 4 basic attacks, as you increase in level your moveset will upgrade at Lv.4 and again at Lv.7 which also has an updated weapon design for the next area you go into after reaching Lv.7, at Lv.10 your Musou Attack will always be a True Musou regardless how much health you have. There is quite a bit to cover, but it should be nice and neat with little clutter for the information you may want.

+ Deployable Officers
There are in total 200 officers you can choose to bring with you along the battlefield, for a list of all the officers refer to [[[https://1drv.ms/x/s!AsI63GpqhuzqibUZ4DxB4mcnxbL2gQ | this Excel sheet]]]. They are listed in order of
* The playable 42 officers in index order (2-43)
* The regular generics by level in alphabetical order (44-178)
* The special 4 generic ladies (179-182)
* The Samurai Warriors cast in index order (183-201)
Each officer has a unit type they will bring to battle as well as possibly having an element that they do for their charge attacks. The letter grade for Deployable Officers translate into ability points which are given for every level they and you gain at the end of a stage with every 100 points combined giving your character +1 to that base stat permanently. The amount given is translated as such
* D = Lv x 10
* C = Lv x 12
* B = Lv x 14
* A = Lv x 16
* S = Lv x 18
* SS = Lv x 20
The character you control will always count as having SS in all 4 stats, if you end up with #98 it will not give your character an extra base stat it must be over #00.

If your birthplace or weapon type match with the Deployable Officer their level will be reduced by 1, if both match it will be reduced by 2. The lowest possible level is 1. Officers can come with up to 3 additional effects to aid during battle which come in the form of Ability, Boost & Skill.
