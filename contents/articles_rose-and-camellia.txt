@title: Rose and Camellia Review
@author: Perseonn Balthasaar
@updated: 2014-05-16

You ever think about action-packed visual novel? If that thought ever cross your mind once, here's a game you might want to check out. It's called Rose and Camellia, a flash-based visual novel available from http://nigoro.jp/ja/games/rose/


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/title.jpg |title=Title screen.]]


The game's music and style will remind you of DOS-based visual novel, especially Nocturnal Illusion.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/opening.jpg |title=Opening screen.]]


The story is about Reiko who married Shunshuke, the eldest son from House of Camellia. Unfortunately, Shunshuke died on the next day. Due to this, Reiko was bullied from the other member of the House of Camellia. She got angry. She took the rose Shunshuke gave her and declared war against the House of Camellia. Since she is the bride of the eldest son, she intended to take control of the House of Camelia. The whole game is her battle.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/battle.jpg |title=Battle begin!]]


But wait... did I mention this is a slapping battle? Yes, slapping.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/criticalhit.jpg |title=This whole game is kinda hard to swallow.]]


The control is easy. Swing the mouse in the direction of your opponent's face to attack, swing mouse in the direction of your neck to dodge.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/gethit.jpg |title=Taking a slap like a lady.]]


The most challenging part of this game is not to take too many hits, since you don't recover from each fight, and there's a total of 5 opponents.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/win.jpg |title=Winning a match is accompanied by a dramatic echo of the last slap.]]


I won't spoil much more than this. It's a short game, and the rest of the House of Camelia member need to be seen by your own eyes to be believed.

====

[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/title.jpg |title=Layar judul.]]


Tampilan dan musik dari game ini akan mengingatkan anda dengan visual novel era DOS.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/opening.jpg |title=Benar-benar mirip DOS.]]


Rose and Camellia bercerita tentang Reiko yang menikah dengan putra pertama Keluarga Camelia, Shunsuke. Sayangnya, Shunshuke meninggal satu hari setelah mereka menikah. Karena statusnya, Reiko diganngu oleh anggota keluarga Camelia. Ini membuat Reiko marah. Dengan menggunakan mawar pemberian Shunshuke, Reiko mengumumkan perang dengan keluarga Camelia. Ia menginginkan kuasa atas keluarga Camelia, meningat ia adalah istri dari putra tertua keluarga Camelia. Game ini adalah pertarungan Reiko melawan anggota keluarga Camelia.



[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/battle.jpg |title=Semuanya dimulai dari sini!]]


Tapi tunggu, pertarungan yang dimaksud bukanlah pertarungan dengan senjata tajam, maupun senjata api.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/criticalhit.jpg |title=TAMPAR!]]


Kontrol game ini sederhana. Gerakkan maouse ke wajah musuh untuk menyerang, gerakkan mouse ke arah leher Reiko untuk menghindar.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/gethit.jpg |title=Kalau tidak menghindar, ya kena tampar.]]


Tantangan di game ini adalah melawan 5 musuh beruntun tanpa disembuhkan. Usahakan tidak terkena tamparan terlalu banyak sampai musuh terakhir.


[[include common:image-css |ratio=4x3 |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:roseandcamellia/win.jpg |title=Tamparan akhir selalu bergema-ma-ma-ma...]]


Jika anda penggemar visual novel, game ini layak dicoba. Anggota lain dari Keluarga Camelia perlu disaksikan sendiri untuk dipercaya.
