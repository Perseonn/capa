@title: Dynasty Warriors
@author: Buster Wolf
@updated: 2025-04-06

The Dynasty Warriors series originally started as a fighting game which the rest of the series did not follow through with, this guide is meant to help with the move list of each playable character so you know what button combinations to press to get your desired attacks. This guide is meant for the US version, but note that if you are playing on the Japanese version [[span class="controller"]]C[[/span]] & [[span class="controller"]]D[[/span]] have their commands swapped, this is included for codes.

+ Basic Commands
||~ Command ||~ Description ||
|| [[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]] || A horizontal swipe. ||
|| [[span class="controller"]]B[[/span]] || A vertical attack or a thrust. ||
|| [[span class="controller"]]C[[/span]] || Defends against [[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]] swipes. ||
|| [[span class="controller"]]D[[/span]] || Defends against [[span class="controller"]]B[[/span]] swipes. ||
|| [[span class="controller"]]C+D[[/span]] || Side step. ||
|| [[span class="controller"]]A+B[[/span]] || Will do a throw from the front only. ||
|| [[span class="controller"]]**&**[[/span]]/[[span class="controller"]]**@@*@@**[[/span]]/[[span class="controller"]]**(**[[/span]][[span class="controller "]]+A[[/span]]/[[span class="controller "]]B[[/span]] || A lunging attack that can also be used to pursuit a downed enemy. ||
|| [[span class="controller qcf"]]+A+C[[/span]] or [[span class="controller qcf"]]+B+D[[/span]] || On the bottom of the screen a bar will fill up, when it is full you are able to perform your characters musou attack. If your health is Red and the bar is full it will be a longer combo. ||
