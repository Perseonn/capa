Destiny of an Emperor is an RPG loosely based off of the Romance of the Three Kingdoms novel, hearing that you probably though KOEI made this, but no this was in fact made by Capcom in 1989. As a Dynasty Warriors fan I will try to point out any naming mistakes on Capcom's side that I know (there are quite a lot) and who certain generals are meant to be, I won't name every single one mainly those you fight throughout the story and those as recommendations for your party. It should be noted that chapters are not announced in-game, but I will tell you when you hit chapter endpoints.

+ Party
A thing to note about the party size is that the max you can have with you is 7 characters, if you have 5 or lower while having a tactician set it will place them at the lowest possible slot, with 6 or 7 they will not be in the battle. Due to a chest bug you should never place a tactician or any high INT generals in 7th with only 2 certain situations allowing you to do so with safety due to how tactic learning is programmed.
+ Generals of Interest
After clearing a story line battle certain generals will be recruitable to join your ranks, any particular one I feel are worthwhile will have their in-game name in **bold** to let you know to try and get them after you clear the castle/fortress they are guarding. Generals you can recruit will always be captured after a random battle once their station has been taken resulting in 4 outcomes when you try to convert them.
* Outright refusal
* Will join for a magnificent horse (Steed)
* Will join for Gold
* Will join for free
+ Stats
||~ Stat ||~ Effect ||
|| STR || The higher the value the higher damage that can be done with physical attacks. ||
|| INT || The higher the value the higher damage can be done with tactics, also influences likeliness that certain tactics will land against lower INT targets. ||
|| A.P || The attack power of the generals weapon for physical attacks. ||
|| A.C || The defense the general has against physical attacks based on their equipped armor, the value for how much damage is reduced is this number divided by 256. ||
|| Soldier || This is effectively the generals HP that other RPG's would denote, when this reaches 0 that general can no longer be used in battle until revived with a Resurrect. ||

++ Tactics
Tactic damage is based on whatever is higher between the generals STR or INT divided by 256, accuracy however is based on the difference between the INT of the user and the target.
||~ INT difference ||~ Accuracy ||
|| 100+ || 15/16 (93%) ||
|| 50-99 || 12/16 (75%) ||
|| 10-49 || 8/16 (50%) ||
The lower the difference including negatives the worse your chances are so don't bother if the difference is worse than above.

+ Tactics List
Every level you gain that gives a learnable tactic will award with 3-5 MTP if you meet the requirement with one of your members. Placing a high INT general as your tactician will give the entire party access to every tactic they have available for use, higher level tactics will often replace lower ones once they are learned/are usable so you may want to use a weaker tactician for specific tactics after level 31 where you learn the last one. Incase of a tactic you don't learn (Fu Bing for example) with your current tactician, but later switch to one with more INT that would be able to will update the list as if they were around to learn it, you won't get the MTP if it was missed.

+ Ch. 1 The Yellow Scarves Rebellion (Yellow Turbans)
++ Luo Song Village
* You start with [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Liu_Bei Liu Bei], [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Guan_Yu Guan Yu] & [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Zhang_Fei Zhang Fei] inside Liu Bei's house, leave his house and look for Zhang Shi Ping (Zhang Shiping) who has a horse nearby and speak with him to receive 200 Gold & 1,000 Rations, without rations you will slowly lose soldiers as you walk around outside of towns so make sure to get this. You will generally get more than you need from defeating bosses so you should never need to buy rations in towns.
* Hire Song Ren & Song Yong, they are mainly extra bodies to take hits for now.
* Equip Liu Bei with the Flail and pass the Dagger to Song Ren, equip Guan Yu with the Flail and pass his Robe to Liu Bei, equip Zhang Fei with the Flail and pass his Robe to Song Ren. To equip gear go to the ITEM menu and highlight the member then choose the desired gear and select EQUIP, if you don't see * next to it it won't count as equipped.
* Rearrange the party in soldier count order, to check the soldier count of your party press Select to see how many everyone has. To rearrange the party pick FORMATION then ORDER and select the general to put them in the order you select.
* Leave Luo Song Village and head West along the road to Xu Zhou.
++ Xu Zhou Castle
* Xu Zhou is a Gullwing point, the Inn is the first building from the entrance.
* Behind it is the Billet where you can add and remove members from your party (with few exception) or fire generals you no longer have a need for.
* The building to it's East is the Provision shop, this is the only time I'll mention if as you should never have a need to use it, but you can exchange money for Food.
* The building behind them is the Historian, this is where you'll save your game, the Invite command doesn't have a use for now.
* The building in the Northeast is the Armory where you'll buy weapons and armor, buy a Bandana for each member with equipment and equip them except Song Ren.

++ Qing Zhou Castle
**Boss**
[https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Zhang_Liang Zhang Liang] + Rebel Force x2
Reduce the Rebel Forces soldier count under 100, afterward concentrate on Zhang Liang.

After this battle you should learn Lian Huo which is a fire tactic, it can deal around 40-90 damage to one general. Though if you chose to grind a level Liu Bei & Mi Zhe would have it for use during the boss battle.
* Don't talk to the NPC right at the entrance.
* Enter the second house to the East from the entrance and speak with the old man to recruit Chen Deng ([https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Chen_Dao Chen Dao]) and make him the tactician which you do by going into FORMATION and then choosing STRAT. and then pick the general in question who must have more INT than STR to be a tactician, also pass the Dagger from Mi Zhe to Chen Deng. 
* Rest at the Inn which is the house West near the NPC by the entrance then talk to the him after resting.

**Boss**
Ma Yuan Yi ([https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Ma_Yuanyi Ma Yuanyi]) + Rebel Force x4
Have Mi Zhe use Lian Huo on Ma Yuan Yi until you run out of TP while the rest of your generals lower the Rebel Forces below 100, afterward focus fire from top to bottom.

After this battle you should learn Wuo Jian which is special tactic, it reduces the damage from enemy fire tactics by half. You also learn of [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Zhang_Bao_(Yellow_Turbans) Zhang Bao]'s whereabouts. Head back to Xu Zhou and buy a Bandana for Chen Deng then rest up at the Inn and save your progress at the historian, afterward leave Xu Zhou and travel West along the road to Mt. Da Xing.

* You can talk to the NPC's to learn where the final brother Zhang Jao ([https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Zhang_Jiao Zhang Jiao]) is located.
* Enter the big tent in the center and CHECK in the top right tile to get an Ax (A.P 20), give this to Zhang Fei and pass his Flail to Chen Deng and pass his Dagger to Mi Zhe.

++ Tia Man Xia
**Boss**
Zhang Jao, Zheng Mao ([https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Deng_Mao Deng Mao]), **Han Zhong** + Rebel Force x2
Focus your physical attackers on Zhang Jao while having Mi Zhe use Wuo Jian on the first turn, have someone reduce the Rebel Forces to have them go under 100 soldiers. Mi Zhe's following turns should have him cast Lian Huo on Zheng Mao until you reduce him to under 100 soldiers then work on Han Zhong, when that is done until you run out of TP then take them out from top to bottom. Should you happen to have Chi Xin from being Lv 5 save at least 3 TP to recover soldiers incase someone gets low.
* If you didn't already learn it by now you should have Shui Tu which is a water tactic, unfortunately it can only be used if there is a source of water within 4 spaces of the battle, it does deal 50-100 damage to one general. It is stronger than Lian Huo, but has limited use due to not many battles being near water.
* Talking to the NPC's inside reveal that the Yellow Scarves retook Qing Zhou and that it's best to attack them from behind, however one of their generals Han Zhong has to be bribed for his help.
* The tent in the West has a treasure chest containing an Ax, give this to Guan Yu and pass down his Flail to Mi Zhe.
* You will want to find Han Zhong in a random battle and recruit him to your side which will help doing the easy way of retaking Qing Zhou. When you recruit him pass Chen Deng's equipment to Han Zhong.

You have two options on how to deal with retaking Qing Zhou.

**Qing Zhou Castle Retake head-on**
Zhang Jao, Zhang Bao, Zhang Liang, Zheng Mao, Cheng Yuan Zhi
You can do the hard way by attacking it head on, which I don't advise. Should you want to do so fill your inventory for every one with Elixir A's as much as possible, this'll restore 100 soldiers should they get low. You will want to focus on Zheng Mao first then Cheng Yuan Zhi using Lian Huo with Guan Yu & Chen Deng before they use Wuo Jian, when you have 4 TP left set up your own Wuo Jian. When left down to just the 3 brothers go for Zhang Liang first then go for Zhang Jao ending with Zhang Bao. Zhang Jao & Bao have Chi Xin which they have a chance to use when they lose half of their soldiers, luckily they will only use it on themself and not each other so keep hacking at them until you eventually come out victorious.

It is not worth the effort as you spend more gold than you gain from doing this one.

* You'll notice a cave behind the which leads to a mini dungeon, put Han Zhong in the lead and talk to the guard to make a bridge over, you can choose to go back and put Han Zhong in the Billet or continue forward.
* Along the way you should learn Chi Xin which is a healing tactic that can restore up to 90 soldiers.

**Chapter Finale**
Zhang Jao, Zhang Bao, Zhang Liang
With less generals to worry about for this one you'll want your physical attackers to go after Zhang Jao first this time with someone casting Wuo Jian and Mi Zhe casting Lian Huo on Zhang Liang until they cast Wuo Jian. You should have Chi Xin by now and you'll want to save 3 TP incase someone needs healing. After Zhang Jao goes down focus on Zhang Bao then Zhang Liang.

**Note:** If you want all possible MTP there is a specific tactic learned at Lv 12 called Fu Bing that only Liu Bei would be able to learn, under normal non glitchy methods this MTP will always be lost as the time you would reach that level you don't have anyone with 220 INT to learn it, it's not too big of a deal if you miss it, but it's there if you want it.
++ Xu Zhou Castle (revisit)
* After your victory over the Yellow Scarves make your way to Xu Zhou and speak to Tao Qian.
* As promised Tao Qian will offer Liu Bei to rule in his stead, while you have the option to say No it's a but thou must situation and it'll infinitely loop until you say Yes.
* Liu Bei will take the throne and will be the ruler as such he will no longer be traveling with the party.
* A messenger from [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Cao_Cao Cao Cao] will mention that [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Dong_Zhuo Dong Zhuo] is causing chaos in Luo Yang and our aid is required to quell him.
* Since Liu Bei will no longer join us, his son [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Liu_Feng Liu Feng] will take his place, the equipment Liu Bei had will be passed down to him so make sure to equip his items.
+ Ch. 2 The Tyrant Dong Zhuo
* Before setting off to Fan Shui Guan (Fan Shui Gate) sell the your extra gear at the Merchant then go to the Armory to buy a Leather for Guan Yu and rearrange your party if you haven't already.
* From Tia Man Xia head around the mountain side to get to Fan Shui Guan, if you didn't learn the following tactic yet do so.
Cheng Nei is a special tactic, this will remove castle defense bonuses that bosses get during battle. This has no effect when used against you so enemy generals throw away a turn casting this.
++ Fan Shui Guan
* On the outside [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Ce Sun Ce] (this should be his father [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Jian Sun Jian]) will mention that they were defeated trying to pass through Fan Shui Guan due to [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Yuan_Shu Yuan Shu] not giving needed supplies. **[https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Hua_Xiong Hua Xiong]**, Li Su + Rebel Force x3

Have your physical attackers focus on Hua Xiong and if any Rebel Force isn't under 100 do so with Mi Zhe & Chen Deng's physical attack otherwise have your spare cast Lian Huo on Hua Xiong while keeping enough TP to cast Chi Xin. * After rearranging your party leave Fan Shui Guan and make your way to Hu Lao Guan (Hu Lao Gate) by heading West, check the space 8 tiles West and 1 South of Fan Shui Guan to get Chi Tu Ma (Red Hare). You are told about this later so you can choose to take it or wait to be told about it, the character who has this in their inventory will have their agility boosted by 50% giving them better chances to go first in battle.

++ Hu Lao Guan
**Boss**
[https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Lu_Bu Lu Bu], **[https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Li_Ru Li Ru]** + Rebel Force x2
While normally I would advise to lower the soldiers of the Rebel Forces under 100, Lu Bu is priority number 1 as he is the most powerful general in the game while having pretty low INT to deal good damage with Lian Huo against him. Due to Li Ru having Ye Huo the next tier for fire tactics get Wuo Jian up asap and have someone case Cheng Nei to get rid of their castle defense. After Lu Bu is out of the picture send everyone after Li Ru saving your TP for Chi Xin.

You should learn Qi Shou which is a special tactic, which increases the agility of the general it is used on.

* The NPC's here give clues on how to get Lu Bu to defect to your side as well as Cao Cao being around in one of the tents.
* The tent East of the main tent with Cao Cao has a set of 3 pots check the top left pot for another Ax which should be your 5th and last one for the entire party.

You may also want to get 2 Leather's from the Armory, if you have enough for a third that's fine to.

* Before continuing to the next area you will want to get the generals Tai Si Ci & Lu Sun to replace Ma Chao & Huang Zhong respectively so Gullwing to Jin Du and walk around the hills until you get these two.
* After getting these generals Gullwing to Ji Zhou to rest up then buy Power Pills if you used any and 3 Gullwings using one to port back to Jian Ye and rearrange your party and equipment to meet the following.
* You will want characters whose INT is higher than 167 to lessen the risk for An Sha's landing, Tai Si Ci's STR & soldier count make up for his lower INT. Another thing to note is that during this chapter any generals you defeat at their post will not be available for recruiting until after the chapter is over.

The party I have is Tai Si Ci, Zhao Yun, ☆Zhu Ge Liang, Lu Sun, Gan Ning, Ma Dai, Zhou Yu.
* At Jin Du head East and follow the path South hugging the Northern wall eventually finding a gate, you will want Zhu Ge Lian in the active party with Zhou Yu as the tactician.

After the battle Si Ma Yi ([https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Sima_Yi Sima Yi]) will arrive seemingly to help Cao Pi, but instead betray him and gives his head to you while taking control of Wei. An unblockable thunder magic will happen knocking your party out sending you back to Liu Bei.
+ Ch. 9 Unification

++ Ru Nan West Cave
image map
* This is the last dungeon in the game while also housing the best equipment such as the Halberd. It has 255 A.P, and while it may seem like a slight upgrade from Qing Long & Nu Long (240), Halberd will deal double damage by comparison gives this to Zhao Yun and pass weapons down in STR order. Another legendary sword you will find is Qing Guang, it is equal in A.P to Qing Long & Nu Long so give this to Xia Hou Dun and pass his previous one to Zhu Ge Liang. None of the other treasures are really noteworthy so make your way to the other entrance.
* Once outside on the Western side heard North and cross the bridge and head West until you see another bridge with some forts, heal with Elixir D's if you have any to spare and make Zhou Yu the tactician so Zhu Ge Liang can be in the party.

++ Chin Castle
**Boss**
Xun Huo ([https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Xun_Yu Xun Yu]), [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Li_Dian Li Dian], [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Wang_Shuang Wang Shuang] + Rebel Force x2

++ Rebuilt Luo Yang
These are the last battles of the game so feel free to go all out with your TP, you can rest at Chin to the South if you need to get your TP back. Have Zhu Ge Liang replace Xia Hou Dun and make Zhou Yu the tactician before starting.

**Gate #2**

**Finale**
Si Ma Yi, [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Yu_Jin Yu Jin], [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Wei_Xu Wei Xu], [https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Cheng_Yu Cheng Yu], Xu Zhu
Have everyone cast Bei Ji on themselves, if Ji Mian is down aim for Cheng Yu first using Power Pills on him, when he's down whittle down Si Ma Yi next and aim to take him down using Power Pills. Afterward take the rest out in order of Xu Zhu, Yu Jin and Wei Xu.

After the battle head to the Historian and invite Liu Bei to Luo Yang and then talk to him in the palace and enjoy the ending.
