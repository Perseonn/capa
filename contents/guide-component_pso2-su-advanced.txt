++ Harmonizer (タクト //Takt//)
[[div class="row"]]
[[image info-2-takt.avif | Takts]]
[[/div]]
For most of the time, sticking to your regular harmonizer is fine, but higher rarity harmonizer has additional effects. Harmonizers are handy, for one, they don't benefit a +35 grind as +30 is fine. This applies to even Rykros Staff (it affects Rykros, but hardly significant).
