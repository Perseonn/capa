|pet-name=Marron (Marron)
|pet-description=A defensive pet with self-explosion ability. Marron is best used when your enemy are overly aggressive, like Dragon. Once was popular pet before damage cap was implemented. With damage cap in place, Marron Strike's capped 1M damage does lower DPS that its required setup. Using Marron for Strike attack only will lower their DPS a lot; consider using the strike as a bonus damage and focus on using Curse and Break as primary DPS.

Marron's size has 3 stages, which powers up their Strike. The 1st stage is default; Marron will always be on 1st stage if you summon them. The 2nd stage is reached after 3 hits, in which Marron becomes big. The 3rd stage is reached after 9 hits, in which Marron becomes even bigger with flames coming out of their body. You have 15 seconds to perform Marron strike before Marron explodes on your hand and reduce your HP to a fraction.

**Unlike Melon, Marron has limited use on regular EQs.** Its overreliance on Marron Strike 3 to do damage become it's main weakness. Its main combo (Curse-Break) is only rated at 73, way lower than the standard of Jinga. It's niche, however, is in solo "hard difficulty" quest which features multi-hit bosses, i.e. Phaleg, Omega Masquerade, and Shiva. In those quest, you can throw Marron Strike 3 in under 30s and swap to another Marron. This increases their DPS potential closer to a Maxed Rowdy Redran.
