@title: Ther Melian RPG Progress (Prologue to Chapter 1)
@author: Perseonn Balthasaar
@updated: 2016-05-28

A couple of years ago, I was asked to join a collaboration to create a prequel game to a best-selling fantasy novel called Ther Melian (still local, as of this article). There was only three of us: Bunny, the franchise owner and script writer; Panda, a seasoned game developer, and myself, an RPG Maker hobbyist. We agreed to use RPG Maker VXAce, but things went south.

+ Background 
We laid groundwork to the game and put as many text from the script as possible. Panda was working on the script, while I was working on the database. We were on the right pace, but life rears its ugly head. Panda was sent to Malaysia to attend a training, while I was stuck teaching so many hours to meet ends. This left the game in limbo and I nearly forgot about it, until RPG Maker MV came out.

RPG Maker MV (2015) rekindles the sideview battle previously seen on RPG Maker 2003, roughly 12 years ago. I was overjoyed and think to rekindle Ther Melian again. However, MV was a mere infant compared to the extensive plug-in library for VXAce (which is an upgrade of VX, a 2007 software). Continuing as it is, MV lacks the vision I have so I laid in wait and begin to create a reusable database and framework, dubbed "Dream Port Engine".

On May 2016, just as my semester come to an end, I noticed that MV has gained a considerable number of plug-ins. I decided to continue Ther Melian alone, with the completed script that Bunny left. In here, I will put the progress comparing the abandoned VXAce to MV.

++ Gardian Sanctum
The sanctum was massively revamped in order to look elven (elvarish, elvaren?). The concept was based on Heroes VI Sanctuary. I wanted to make a lotus pathway instead of paving, but that's just impossible seeing how scarce the lotus brush is. Valadin was also made not to sound over-eager.
