Welcome one and all to this Weapon Stat Guide! As many of you know the effects in SC2 are ambiguous as hell and don't fully tell the story (effects) right, some even lie about what effect it has and others don't even tell you anything (Legendary Weapons) . I have taken the liberty to deeply uncover the mysteries of each weapon so before I start with the weapons here are effects you will be seeing, be careful as it may get confusing later on down the line so check these often.

This guide was previously published on Another Stage of History.

+ Legend and Notes
* Console specific characters are excluded from the list.
* Throws and Unblockable damage is dependent on your weapons attack, unlike SC3 and SC4.
* As with all Soul series, higher attack number means higher damage dealt and higher defense number means higher damage taken. Example: Attacker with 100% damage weapon versus defender with 70% defense will mean that the attacker will generally do 100%*70% = 70% damage against the defender.
* Defense Written as 100/(**XXX**) 100: Weak defense against single strikes. The first strike of an attack will do what is in the parenthesis any other hits resulting in a combo will do damage based on the outside, single hits do more damage than a comboed hit. Example: Xianghua's AAB with the No Name will do 84%,70%,70% against a Talim holding Wind Guide.
* Attack Written as (**1XX**) 100/100: Strong single strikes. First hit will do more damage than the rest of a combo
* Attack written as (**SC1XX**) 100/100: Meaning: Soul Charged single strikes do more damage. This is really only here cause it is said on the weapon for whatever reason...
