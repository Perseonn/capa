|pet-name=Redran
|pet-description=A long-range pet that can tear multi-part enemies, such as Ragne and Vibras, quickly. On the reverse side, they also can tear down Redran quickly. Redran has the highest Pwr among all pets, but also the lowest Def of all pets, with about 120 HP less than Synchro and only 60 DEF better.

Redran, along with Sally and Popple, can jam. Pressing a PA button at the same time when they lose target will cause them to skip the said PA, but will still cost PP. Mind the timing between PAs.

I believe Redran is a portmanteau of Laser Dragon (**レ**ーザー**ドラ**ゴ**ン**), thus their name should be Ladran (read: leɪdræn). Would be too difficult to read, though.

**Redran has minimal use on EQ outside seasonal, defense, and endless quests; especially because they tend to run out of PP quickly. In addition, Redran is hard-pressed to secure the number of kills it needed in UH fields, further hampering its usage.**
