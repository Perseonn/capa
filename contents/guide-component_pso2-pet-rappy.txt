Rappy invokes the Magikarp Power: its stat before L120 are not impressive, and has 250 points to fill on each stat of Pet Plus; Level 100 and 120 Rappy has attack differences of almost 400 points. They notably have strongest normal attacks, albeit smaller hit box. This is both blessing and a curse since it is more accurate to hit a weak point, but easier to miss moving enemies. They are also the only pet to have a healing PA.

Rappy is the best pet to abuse Braver sub's Attack Advance. Stacking Attack Advance and Spirited Parfait turns Rappy's normal attack into a PA, essentially allowing you to win boss fights by just holding Normal Attack Button.
