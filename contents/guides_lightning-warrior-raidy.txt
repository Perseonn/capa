@author: Buster Wolf
@updated: 2018-10-09

Lightning Warrior Raidy is a first person dungeon crawler RPG, the game is pretty basic in its mechanics so there's not much to worry about. You can move either from using the arrow keys or keys on the number pad, Up and Down will move you one tile in the direction you face while Left and Right will turn you in that direction. During battle Raidy can use her special attack Thunder Slash when you click the Thunder Bolt icon during battle, damage dealt is based on how much MP was used which will always be half of your current. There is no point to the Defend command as damage does not get reduced unless you want to lose to a boss for the lose scene. Running away from battle will move you back to the tile you were at before you got into a battle, if you find an enemy too tough you can choose to run. The material in this game is not meant players who are underage.

The amount of EXP monsters will give is **EXP - Raidy's level** for example Wererat will give you 20 EXP when you beat one at Level 1 (21-1) so you will get less EXP as you level up. Suggested level is mainly for a balanced playthrough not too high and not too low.

+ Stats
**HP:** Your health. If it runs out, it's Game Over.
Whenever you gain a level, max HP increases by a random number ranging from 10 to 21.

**MP:** Your mana. Half the current amount is used whenever you use Thunder Slash.
Whenever you gain a level, max MP increases by a random number ranging from 10 to 21.

**AP:** Your accuracy stat. It determines how often your attacks hits.
Whenever you gain a level, AP increases by 1 point.

**PP:** Your evasion stat. It determines how often you evade enemy normal attacks.
Whenever you gain a level, PP increases by 1 point.

**DMG:** Your damage stat. It determines the amount of damage your attacks do.
Whenever you gain a level, DMG increases by 4 points.

**DEF:** Your defense stat. It determines the amount of damage you receive from enemy normal attacks.
Whenever you gain a level, DEF increases by 3 points.

+ Floor 3
Suggested Level: 11
This floor has a teleport maze you must get through which are number and color coded on the map. At this point Light Heal Potions are no longer needed (and will no longer be given in chests or enemy drops) so feel free to use them all. Due to the Ghost enemy you may want to keep your MP low until you are in front of the boss door. This floor can be the toughest to get passed due to the enemies mostly the Lamia.

**Enemies**

|| Cerberus || 120 || 82 ||
|| Lamia || 140 || 93 ||
**Ghost** - Has the fixed damage skill Cold Hand which will drain 11 MP.
**Cerberus** - Has the fixed damage skill Breath which will do 36 damage.
**Lamia** - The hardest monster here even over leveled due to her high evade stat, her normal attack has a chance to Poison and she has the fixed damage skill Spell which will do 39 damage.

**Suggested event sequence**
1. Find the Charmed Man, watch the scene to get the Jail Key.
2. Use the Jail Key inside the empty cell to get the Morion Earrings.
3. Find the Bastard Sword (AP -3, DMG +20). You may not want to equip it right away since it tends to miss a lot until you reach level 14.
4. Find the 3rd Floor Map.
5. Defeat the Boss.

**Final Boss**
Sex Demon Cubust
HP: 1600
Suggested Level: 26

Cubust is no pushover as his physical attacks will hurt up to 40 at the max level (35), 80 should he get a critical hit. Use Thunder Slash to soften him up a bit then proceed to use physical attacks when it can do more than Thunder Slash. Since this is the battle you can use your Heavy Heal Potions and Mental Recovery potions as often as you need though make sure that you heal when you have double the highest damage he can deal incase he gets a lucky critical.

After defeating Cubust a cutscene will play which will lead into two decisions

**Give in to the pleasure** which will lead to the bad ending (same scene as losing against Cubust unless...)
**Resist** which will lead to the good ending

+ Extra Info
++ Alternate Bad Ending
You will get this if you lose against each boss (auto-defeat doesn't count) during your climb and choose **Give in to the pleasure** after beating Cubust.
