@title: Phantom Brave
@author: Buster Wolf
@updated: 2026-09-25

Phantom Brave is a separate Strategy RPG IP from Nippon Ichi Software, if you are used to their quirky and zany humor you won't find as much here as this has a darker tone than they are known for. This was released on the PS2 in 2004, then it was later ported to the Wii (We Meet Again) in 2009 and then again on PSP (The Hermuda Triangle) both of which added Another Marona story and extra characters. The version this guide is based on is the Steam port Phantom Brave PC though most of it should still be doable other versions.

Those following along (specially Steam players) the suggested order is Marona's Story without doing the Ex maps, transferring to Another Marona after you finish Marona's story, then back to Marona's story and do the EX Maps after finishing her story again.

+ Suggested starting party
Before starting the game has a variety of units available, you will want to keep your main party low as you progress.

* Marona - You have no choice as she's always available and doesn't leave the field after a certain number of turns. Unlike other units she'll gain a percentage of EXP whenever a stage is cleared regardless how early or late in the game it is. The item you will want for heFr is a Vase though a Cane will suffice until they are available to specialize in her Healing special SP.
* Ash - He will stay in battle for a long time and has a unique power boost on his last turn for massively increased damage. He is good as a warrior type character though you may want to focus more on Energy attack specials, due to his unique special he'll gain from leveling up.
* A secondary physical fighter - Fighter, Valkyrie, Owl Knight & Owl Sentry are all good choices for a generic physical unit, they all have a remove stat of 5 so they will last long on the field, unlike Ash these you'll want to focus on Physical attack specials.
* 2 casters - You have a variable choice of magic units between Witch, Owl Mage and Putty Mage. Of the three, Witch is the most powerful having higher Int, while Owl & Putty mages are even in Int; Owl Mage is the fastest while Putty Mage is the slowest. The Fusionist is also good if you want to use Nature based specials, though look for ones that go off INT from Weeds, Flowers & Sunflowers.
* Bottlemails - These quirky little guys are the best unit to steal and throw enemy items with. They also have the best chance to bring confined items home when they run out of turns. They can be found in Random Dungeons with Weird as the enemy type, you can also get one for free from stacking high enough on the big tree next to Marona's house. Bring at least 2 so you can take some rare or powerful items with you.

++ Utility Units
These are units that have an external benefit for being on the island, while some are recommend to not be used for battle their services have better payoffs the higher their level so Confine them in safety so they can get EXP for showing up.

* Merchant - You can buy weapons from her that are around her level. After battle she has a chance to bring some Bordeaux. Her wares also increase in rank and title quality as her levels increase as follows.

* Healer/Owl Sage - They heal and revive characters similar to the Hospital feature in other games.
* Witch - She can arrange your specials and delete ones you don't want, her level doesn't matter.
* Dungeon Monk - He can make random dungeons which are useful for endgame leveling, he can get you out of dungeons he made with his Return special. It can be transferred to others, but will cost much much more if not used by him.
* Blacksmith/Putty Smith - They are used to level up items and unlock specials by using Mana gained by the item, if the item is higher than the smith's level it will be increasingly more expensive in cost.
* Fusionist/Putty Shaman - They combine units and items for fusion using Mana, used for passing specials. After battle they have a chance to bring some Mana to anything.
* Old Man - After battle he may randomly give EXP to anything.
* Titlist - He can change the of units, items and even random dungeons from the Dungeon Monk. Level doesn't matter for him.
* Granny - After battle she may randomly remove Dark Points from your party.

+ Extra Notes
++ Class Unlocking
Humanoid classes only need to be defeated once in order to unlock them, monster classes on the other hand need to be defeated at least 20 times in order to create them. If there's a particular monster you want to use it would be worth it to repeat the stage they're on first until you can use them. The Summon monster type is one that can't be recruited.

++ Death
There are 2 forms of death in this game, Unconscious which happens when your health goes to 0 and Dead which happens after the unconscious body has been dealt enough damage. Should a character be Unconscious they can be revived mid-battle with the Revive special or after battle at the Healer, however should the Unconscious body be destroyed you not only have a higher cost to pay at the Healer, but also need to sacrifice an item to bring them back. Don't use a Changebook or Egg though, that's for later in the guide.

++ Speed
If you're used to other NIS games you will think speed is worthless, but unlike other NIS games speed actually determines turn order, not what you can dodge. Items that naturally increase speed like Weeds & Trolleys give the biggest boost to speed and are valuable for fusion.

++ Dark Points
Should you accidentally (or incidentally) defeat an ally you will gain Dark Points, with the exception of Marona and Ash the more Dark Points obtained the more likely they will damage allies on the isle. That doesn't sound nice, but if you can get the unit to 100 Dark Points they will get the Blsphm title and the very expensive Dark Eboreus special costing 35 Energy SP. IMPORTANT NOTE, the titles gained while having Dark Points (Unliked and Blsphm) can't be switched at the Titlist, however Granny can remove Dark Points in between battles.

+ Post Game Notes
++ Raising the Level Cap
If you're used to the Disgaea series or other NIS games at the time you would think 9999 is the level cap, this is both true and not at the same time. The starting level cap for units is 100, so how do you increase it? To do so fuse items or other units onto the unit in question at the Fusionist to increase their level cap. The level cap for items tend to be their starting level +10, the absolute level cap is 9999.

++ Passive Specials
These are beneficial effects given to a unit once it is transferred to them, the more useful ones are listed below and with what unit has it to transfer to others via the Fusionist.
||~ Name ||~ Source ||~ Effect ||
|| Exp. Riser || Dungeon Monk (20), Amazon (10), Putty (10) || +1% Exp per level ||
|| Mana Monger || Blacksmith (20), Fusionist (20), Putty Smith (10), Putty Shaman (10) || +1% Mana per level ||
|| Made of Money || Merchant (20), Blob (20) || +1% BOR per level ||
|| Healing Steps || Knight (10), Behemoth (10) || Restore +1% HP per level every turn ||
|| !Healing Birth || Mystic (20), Lizardman (10) || Restore +1% HP per level on Confinement ||
|| !Swift Attack || Archer, Fenrir, Werewolf || Act immediately after Confinement ||
|| !Energy Saver || Soldier || +1 Remove ||
! Do not give Marona these as they do nothing on her, also don't increase Swift Attack or Energy Saver as they do not have increasing effects.

For passing specials to units it is suggested to use a Slime to pass a fusion to before your target unit if the unit in question has B or lower compatibility.

++ Obtaining Titles to pass
To get titles from units or items pick up the unit or item in question and speak with Marona, pick to banish it and she will give a second confirmation. Saying yes will have her banish it and you'll get the title it had added to the list your Titlist can change. This is how you obtain the desired Failure title...that's not a joke btw.

++ Failure Dungeon and Fusion
Find yourself stuck in a particular spot and need a grinding spot? I WOULD recommended Monetopia - Peaceful Hill, but the problem with it is that it takes too long to do and the Bordeux you get is ludicrously high that you'll never run out unless you do so on purpose. Not to mention that it only works on the one character with Big Bang, so what is a better alternative? Random dungeons, more specifically Failure titled dungeons. These are created by a Dungeon Monk and is the best source for high level items. The process goes
* Go to the Dungeon Monk and Create a Dungeon, look for Size: Small, Number of Enemies: Few and Ground: Normal with the highest level possible.
* Go to the Titlist and change the Dungeon to have Failure as it's title, this will massively weaken enemies allowing you to take on powerful enemies with a weak team.
* Go to the Dungeon and complete it.
The highest level dungeon you can make is based on your Dungeon Monk who will gain exp as you complete dungeons they make, also items are not affected by the title so you can get great items that can replace what you are using and also helpful to get items with large amounts mana on them for fusion. 

For failure fusion having 2 of the same item is recommended, you will get a smaller boost using a good title compared to using the Failure title on the one you want to boost. The process goes
* Go to the Titlist and give the item you want to fuse the Failure title.
* Go to the Fusionist and pick your wanted item with the Failure title and the recipient item you are wanting to fuse.
* Pick the parameters and specials you want to pass on and confirm, should you not have enough Mana fuse other unused items until you have enough.
* After the desired fusion go back to the Titlist and give the original title back.
The item should be loads more powerful than it would've been had you done a regular fusion, leveling Fusionist will lower the mana cost.

++ Increasing Equip%
You may notice that all items have a percentage after to their stats which determine how much you are getting while holding them. Some may even have detrimental %'s which cause you to lose stats, though you will never go below 1. Increasing the Equip% can give you a big edge giving more benefits to items that have initial large drawbacks, Confine% also works the same when confining Phantoms to your items. Certain items are best used in fusion to increase Equip% which if yours is naturally under it will continue to rise until you reach it's current %.

Equip% items to look for
||~ Stat ||~ Items (%) ||
|| HP || Bread (100%) ||
|| ATK || Dark Wpn (100%) ||
|| DEF || Rock (85%), Hammer (80%) ||
|| INT || Bomb (80%), Skull (80%), Must-read (70%) ||
|| RES || Star Staff (60%), Dead Staff (60%), SpiritStaff (60%), Must-read (60%), Lthograph (60%) ||
|| SPD || Trolly (90%), Dark Wpn (70%), Weed (70%) ||
The list doesn't include Another Marona exclusive items that have higher Equip% values

To go further beyond you will need to fuse items with the same Equip% value, so for instance to go from Trolly's 90% SPD you need to fuse with another to get a Trolly with 95% SPD. If you are wanting to go higher you will need to fuse 2 more separate Trolly items before fusing them again for 100% and so on and so forth. You will have to continually chain fuse items until you get to your desired % with your main item, regular items increase by 5% per fusion while if you get a Combo item from Random Dungeons they will increase by 10% if they are used last and aren't surpassed by the item fusing with them. The max Equip% is 200%, for Confine% it's 100%, while the max stats are 9,999,999.

++ Reincarnation
Those familiar with Disgaea should know how this works, however there's an extra step. I mentioned in Deaths to not use a Changebook or Egg as a sacrifice to bring a unit back to life, that is because using these items will instead have the character reincarnate. Those on the PS2 version have a limited supply of these while other ports have other ways of obtaining these. Marona, for obvious reasons, is unable to reincarnate.
