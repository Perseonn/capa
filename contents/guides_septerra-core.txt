@author: Perseonn Balthasaar

Septerra Core is an ATB-based RPG and can be considered the western equal of Final Fantasy VII. However, once you moved shell, the game become "Where the heck do I go next" kind of game that pits you against nearly moon-logic on how to trigger the next story event. There's a part that you need to makeshift a boat to cross a lake while you have a ship, or needing laser and not use Runner, your member whose entire repertoire of attacks are laser-based.

If you are brave enough to explore the 7 shells of Septerra Core, prepare for giant amount of tedium.

+ Patches

+ Cheats
* Press F12 and type in {{IMAREALWEENIE}}
 * In battle you can press Ctrl + F3 for instant win.
  * Battles are massive time sinks, save yourself.
 * Press F12 and type in {{ENEMIES}} to show enemy HP and turn gauge. Type again to disable it.
* Press F12 and type in {{MAPMAKER}} to save an image of the entire map as BMP to the installation folder.
* Press F12 and type in {{MAKETHEMSTOPMOMMIE}} to disable enemy encounter, but this includes bosses, so use with care, and a lot of backup saves. RType it again to disable it.
* EXP Cheats, but tread carefully
 * Save before attempting
 * Press F12 and type in {{WTFWWT-BGIOB}}
 * Press F2 to gain 2300 EXP
 * Warning! Pressing F3 will cause Grub and Runner to be added to your party, and can break the game!
 * Restart game to turn off the cheat
* You can use Cheat Engine to speedup the game.
