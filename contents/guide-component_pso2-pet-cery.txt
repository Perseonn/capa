Sally and Popple can jam. Pressing a PA button at the same time when they lose target will cause them to skip the said PA, but will still cost PP. Mind the timing between PAs.

★14 Sally is more durable with higher HP and Def and less PP usage. ★14 Popple has higher ATK and upgraded Popple Plode (chargeable and stronger hit, but lower DPS). There are little reasons to use Sally (even less than Wanda-Jinga or Trim-Aero), so the numbers listed on here are based on Popple.
