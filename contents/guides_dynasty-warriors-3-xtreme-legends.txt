Dynasty Warriors 3: Xtreme Legends is the first ever expansion to a Warriors title, as with all Xtreme Legends expansions it has its own content to separate itself from the [[link /guide:dynasty-warriors-3 | vanilla game]], but to have all the content from the vanilla game Mixjoy is needed.

Three words of warning before beginning
* First if you used Cheat Codes with the vanilla game your save cannot be transferred to Xtreme Legends due to anti cheating measures.
* Second if you have Xtreme Legends and are wondering should you transfer/mixjoy with the vanilla game do so as early as possible, due to the item Musou Armor requirement rank points will get reset back to 0 if you transfer your save. Your stats will be kept though.
* Third all 4th weapons and the old Unique items require the vanilla game via mixjoy which are all under Original Mode, this is also true for those on Complete Edition Remastered.

+ 5th Weapons
New to this expansion are 5th weapons for everyone, unlike the vanilla game all weapons are in a box this time with no Supply Captains to chase as it’s tough enough to do some of these requirements. These are some of the toughest weapons in the series to get and require the following
* Difficulty must be set to Very Hard, Archers and Crossbowmen are very lethal as they have high damage output so watch your surroundings, playable generals will also be using 4th weapons which do include elemental attacks to be weary of.
* 9 of the weapons require Xtreme Mode (this game) while the other 32 require Original Mode (the vanilla game), make sure you are playing the correct mode. These are written as (O) Original, (X) Xtreme in front of the stage name. This holds true if on Complete Edition Remastered where stages requiring Xtreme need the Xtreme tag in Free Mode.
* In addition to the previous elements a new one was added to this game Wind, its effect is to increase juggle height in order to help keep your combo going.

+ Dim Sum & Musou Wine Locations
All stages may have a Dim Sum which increases max Health by 10 and a Musou Wine which increases max Musou by 10 somewhere in the stage, they are found in pots. While they share the same maps as the original, Dim Sum & Musou Wine locations are different, unit placement based on force chosen doesn't matter.

++ Battle of Ru Nan
* **Dim Sum:** Middle ledge on the East castle wall.
* **Musou Wine:** Middle ledge on the West castle wall.

++ Break Limit Items
New to this version is that Blue items have a higher limit they are able to reach, how do we get items to this new cap?
First you will need to be playing on Very Hard, you can do it on Hard, but the requirement is higher at 1,500 combo point. You need to get a combo point score of 1,000 on Very Hard before collecting item bags. So what constitutes a Perfect combo? You need to have a string of unique finishers together within a short time frame. An example of one is

[[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]B[[/span]] ~ [[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]B[[/span]] ~ [[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]B[[/span]] ~ Short jump attack ([[span class="controller"]]C[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]]) ~ [[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]A[[/span]][[span class="controller"]]B[[/span]](*[[span class="controller"]]B[[/span]]) ~ [[span class="controller"]]D[[/span]]
Note that your attack needs to hit someone to continue being counted as a combo point as long as it was a different part of the string. The amount of points you get is not based on the visible number, but what the combo text says counted as followed.

||~ Text Rank ||~ Combo Points given ||
|| Good || 15-25 ||
|| Great || 30-35 ||
|| Perfect || 110-135 ||
So you may score a Great at 90 combo points before it fades, but it will only contribute 30-35 (raises as you get more) to the total score visible at the end of the stage. So you want to aim for Perfects for the most value with your preferred character practicing in Combo Mania. If you aren’t able to score Perfects 4 Greats will roughly equal a Perfect.
