+ Northern Forest (Falthemnar Fenlands)

[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/north-forest.avif |title=The North Forest.]]


The Northern Forest is a relatively small dungeon, serving as a hub between Falthemnar and the Dark Forest. At the end, a boss battle against Alphyn the Electric Beast took place. Like all the previous boss, Alphyn is no pushover, especially since Valadin is weak to thunder. It boasts clock up ability, which increases his turn ratio.

[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/story-5.avif |title=Northern Forest event]]
[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/story-6.avif |title=Alphyn the electric beast.]]


After the battle, Karth and Izahra will leave party. Karth will create a shortcut should player decided to resupply to town.

[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/story-7.avif |title=Battle aftermath.]]
[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/story-8.avif |title=Karth, hinting a shortcut he will make.]]


+ Dark Forest (Misty Moon Labyrinth)

[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/dark-forest.avif |title=The Dark Forest.]]


The dark forest features the same aura as the Atoll of Sorrows. Player must navigate until the find an accessible elvar gate. Being hard as it is, the dungeon will not feature Maximum HP reduction.

[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/story-9.avif |title=First time in dark forest.]]
[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/story-a.avif |title=The accessible elvar gate.]]


Another Alphyn will attack. This is a scripted battle since in no way a Valadin and Ellanese alone can fight the Alphyn, even though system wise it is the same Alphyn. Player will be teleported to the enchanted side of the forest. This is different than the original script in which Valadin and Ellanese jumped off a cliff into a swamp (which  would result in death).

[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/story-b.avif |title=The second alphyn.]]
[[include image |image=http://capa.wikidot.com/local--files/article:ther-melian-5/story-c.avif |title=Escaped to the enchanted labyrinth.]]


The first appearance of Eizen. Different from the script, Eizen is only protected by a barrier instead of a boss. However, the boss will come much later when Valadin decided to come there.
