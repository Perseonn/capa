Disgaea is a strategy RPG series, this one being the first. The purpose of this guide is to list item locations for Main Story, Promotion Exam, Bonus Worlds & Etna Mode in case you are looking for a particular item type to steal with a Thief for those playing ports of this game for completion of the Collection Record at the Save Shop. Unless noted any item they carry can potentially be of any rarity (Common, Rare or Legendary). Do note that some equipment may go by a different name if you are playing the DS version so pay attention to the names. If any unique enemies appear on a map they will be listed first followed by the normal enemies that appear, if a map has a different enemy from the first time it is done they will be listed separately. Endings will also be listed with a link to a YouTube video in case you want to see the ending again, all endings lead to a new cycle regardless which one you get.

Those playing Disgaea 1 Complete have some classes replaced, these are only visual and will still follow what the original class did including needing the Thief to unlock Majin despite being a female now instead of male.

+ Power Leveling
Feel under powered and need a character to gain levels quickly? Certain maps can be done to gain levels quickly to help boost your characters that need an edge in levels.

**3-4 Lavish Hall**
This stage has 6 Brawlers lined up in a wall with their class called Wall of Meat. They will never move or attack regardless of how close you are to them, however they will counter if you do a normal attack in the tile directly in front of them. You can combine them into one high leveled Brawler to gain a lot of EXP if you kill it.

**5-3 Terrible Cold**
This is an interesting level given that there are only two tiles that don't have Invincibility on it, those being your base panel & a lone Green panel on the upper right of the map. This is once again a combine enemies into one for massive exp though the first few times you do this it will take awhile since the combined enemy will be at a pretty high level (for this point) thus you wont be dealing too much damage initially. This works very well with the Triple EXP bill due to only 1 enemy being defeated.

**10-1 Fervent Melody**
Similar to 5-3 this map has a lot of Invincibility tiles, unlike 5-3 however there are a couple of spots in the center of the map that don't have Invincibility which is where you will want to lift the Guardians and combine them from due to this map also having No Lifting. Like 5-3 the Triple EXP bill works well with this map due to only 1 enemy being defeated.

**14-1 Field of Virtue**
If you do not have access to the map below this is the next best place to level grind from the main story. The repeated version has 3 Strigois and 1 Neuntoter who should pose no threat to your party with high level gear on a map filled with the EXP+100% effect.

**Cave of Ordeal 3**
This map has a 3x3 formation of 8 Nataku's and 1 Talos with the entire map covered in an EXP+50% effect. Their DEF and RES is very high, but if you are using Magic (preferably Star-tiers) as long as you stay or move 6 spaces away from them they will not use Magnetic Blast which is their furthest reaching special given their low move of 2.

**Beauty of Evil**
For this you will want to have a Triple EXP bill active before entering to get the most of it. You will want to aim for defeating a Divine Majin first as they give the most experience in the game (next to Item God 2's in the Item World).

**Demonhall Mirror**
After finishing all stages of Demonhall Mirror the final and repeated version will basically be an enhanced Cave of Ordeal 3 with higher leveled Ravana's & Argus.

**Etna Mode+ Cave of Ordeals 3**
The same as Laharl's version of the stage however the Nataku's & Talos will be at much higher levels when you go through Etna Mode as the next cycle. Note that Laharl, Maderas, Gordon, Jennifer, Thursday, Kurtis, Priere & Marjoly can't take advantage of this grinding spot.

+ Etna Mode
In all ports there is an extra story line known as Etna Mode. There are two ways to unlock this either complete the game after reading Etna's diary entries every chapter by finding the hidden switch behind the throne and the other near the skull by the Weapon/Armor shops to open her room near the Prinny in the hall where Longinus is at (the Prinny stands to show you the corner it's in), or on the main menu highlight New Game and press Triangle, Square, Circle, Triangle, Square, Circle, X, for the DS version you press X, Y, B, X, Y, B, A. If you hear Etna's voice that should confirm that it worked.

**Warning:** If you are going to do this after the Main Story make sure your levels are above 260 or you have sufficiently powerful gear as doing this second causes enemies to jump in levels as you go further. For reference Etna mode done first has the highest level non-boss enemy at 40 compared to 260 when doing it second.
