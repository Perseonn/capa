+ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
++* What units / armors should I use?
If you just started the game and have no idea on affixing a unit:
* For current North America version, you can obtain Saiki unit set from L61+ Gigur Gunnegam (ギグル・グンネガム //Giguru Gunnegamu//) or Brissa unit set from L66+ Gwahl Jigmolde (グアル・ジグモルデ //Guaru Jigumorude//). Saiki has more PP where Brissa has a bit more ATK and HP. These two sets are the easiest to obtain.
* You can get 1 Weapons Barrier (サブ／ウェポンズバリア) from clearing Cofy (コフィー //Koffie//)'s quest Training Quest Certification (練習クエスト履修証明). You can utilize all 3 characters to obtain multiple copies of it. Until L75, this is the best unit you can obtain.
* You can get 3 Stella Walls (サブ／ステラウォール) from Lachesis the Title Counter when you have 2 L75 classes.
* You can get Ray units — Circu Ray, Circa Ray, Circ Ray — from Badge and Memory Shop. You need 10 Weapons Badge SP on Shop 1 for each of them.

++* [[# mel-v-tec-mag]] Why not use MEL mag? Doesn't MEL Mag on Fi give 6.5% more damage with all the MEL passives?
In theory where a lot of variables dismissed, yes. However, PSO2's damage factors are actually:
* Stat
* Multipliers
* Attacks or skills used.
* PP circulation, includes max PP, natural PP regeneration, and attack PP regeneration.
* Combo or attack rotation.
* Survival abilities, such as dodge and damage mitigation.
So if you have great stat and multiplier, but lack PP circulation and combo is nothing, you'll have lower DPS.

PP, combo, and survival abilities all depend on the player, so I decided to test this MEL Fi vs TEC Fi myself with the following condition:
* 200 Dex Mag with Megid auto attack, Mag Excite Ring, full mag energy.
 * Dex mag is used because lack of MEL mag.
* Units with total of 180 + 270 ATK.
* Tree Boost and Shifta EX drink is used.
* Jinga A with MEL-SPI Cookie (+120 MEL, +18PP).
* Jinga B with TEC-SPI Cookie (+120 TEC, +18PP).
* Player (Manaka) is a Fem Cast, naturally has higher MEL and lower TEC.
 * Player is a L95/90 SuFi.
 * Player has 284 PP.
 * Player takes all MEL up skills on Fi tree, along with all PP/half/deadline slayers.
 * Player uses Switch Strike when using Jinga A (MEL).
* Player doing 90s of attacking consisting of Jinga Pulsar and Break spam, repeated 4 times (i.e. sample size is 4).
 * Alter Ego is used.
 * Player attacks until PP runs out and let pet normal attacks until PP is full, during normal attack, pet's Shifta is reapplied.
 * All Shifta sympathy requests are fulfilled.
 * At 16s left of Alter Ego cooldown, if Jinga is doing normal attacks, Jinga Pulsar and Break spam will be used for last burst.

The result of average DPS are like this:
||~ Condition ||~ DPS ± St. Dev ||
|| Jinga A + Harmonizer (MEL) || 244K ± 4.8K ||
|| Jinga A + Rykross (MEL) || 322K ± 2.8K ||
|| Jinga B + Harmonizer (TEC) || 235K ± 5.3K ||
|| Jinga B + Rykross (TEC) || 318K ± 6.2K ||
* By comparing average, the MEL and TEC on a regular Harmonizer differs by ~3.8%
 * According to MEDCALC, the significance level is P = 0.0353 (less than 0.05), indicating significant difference.
* On the other hand, the average of MEL and TEC on Rykross is ~1%
 * MEDCALC gives the significance level at P = 0.3584 (more than 0.05), which indicating insignificant difference.

So thus, at same skill level, MEL is only 3.8% better. Further more, when Rykross comes, all MEL-based SuFi will be doing the same damage as a TEC-based SuFi. Considering MEL ability affixes are expensive, using TEC is more reasonable and you can save a lot of Meseta to grab those costumes and lobby actions!

++* What should I use against <Insert Boss Name>?
Wanda or Jinga is always your the first pet that you should use when you are testing new boss or content. They have no real weakness and is suitable for every possible scenario.

++* Wanda or Jinga?
This depends on your playstyle. Wanda has better defense and more efficient Slicer, while Jinga has overall better DPS.

++* Sally (//Cery//) or Popple?
Popple is overall better since it has Charged Popple Plode, a wide area attack; and its main DPS move, Popple Curse, only cost 5 PP.

++* Trim (//Torim//) or Aero?
Trim has slightly better defense, although it doesn't help much. Aero's Charged Shout Spiral 2 have wider area of attack and less PP cost. Since the bulk of Aero's DPS is from Shout Spiral (using 2 seconds Charged Shout Spiral is already better than Shout Slash), having it easier to hit is a big plus. In practice however, both has minimal use.

++* Marron (//Maron//) or Melon?
Neither are practical for mobbing, so let's assume it's for bossing. Marron is more useful if the boss is overly aggressive and have high attack, like Omega Masquerade, Episode 4 Phaleg, and the inhabitants of the solo XQ Phantom. Melon is more useful if the boss isn't aggressive, and is even more useful of both if you have both a ★14 Melon and a Rykros Staff. Conditions aside, Marron needs to take 9 hits to maximize its Strike PA and it will be incapacitated after. Melon needs only 2 hits to maximize its Strike PA, but if you hold the PA button after the throwing animation, you can catch Melon back, like a yo-yo. Both require you to use their Curse attack once every 10 seconds.

++* What's the viability of Normal Attack Rappy?
In advent of Super Spirited Parfait from Primordial Darkness / Last Boss, Rappy could just use it's accurate Normal Attack without using any PAs. The boost from both Super Spirited Parfaits and Spirited Parfait grants 210% Normal Attacks, this boosts it's Normal Attack score from base 46 to ~96.6. Add extra 111% from Ayer Harmonizer, and you'll get 107.2 score. Add extra 108% from Attack Advance Ring, you'll end with 115.8 score

On the other hand, Rykros Rappy Drop, scored at 95 during 60s Alter Ego and 73 during 30s Alter Ego cooldown, this amounts to the score of [60/90 * 95 + 30/90 * 73] = 87.6. If we apply both Super Deadly and Super Doki Doki, this becomes [87.6 + 1.16 * 1.14] = 115.84 score. Add extra 103% from Critical Strike Ring, you'll end with 119.3 score. However, this score is based on ideal condition where the Rappy has infinite PP.

Since the maximum difference is only [119.3 / 115.8] = 3%, it's safe to say that it's a worthwhile trade.

++* Should I have multiple elemental pets?
It's generally not recommended to, especially if you have limited resources.

If you're new, just use whatever parfait you can get from Pietro's COs, Titles, and Seasonal Urgents. Remember that it's better to focus on putting Pancakes first before parfait!

++* What about this @@____@@ Parfait?
* Some parfait have niche use, and isn't compliant to the more general use.
* Any parfait that requires you to have certain % of PP is not recommended since your PP fluctuates easily.
* Any parfait that requires pet to have low HP is not recommended since you'll want pets to have full HP.
* Reckless Parfait (がむしゃらパフェ //Daredevil Parfait//) is not recommended because it greatly reduces PP efficiency. It is better for you to PA more often than using normal attacks more often.



++* Do S Eggs have any difference than regular eggs, apart from color?
No. They are akin to having a shiny Pokemon.


++* Why recommend Spirita Cookies at all? Doesn't Shifta affect pet's total Pwr (ATK)?
* This is my personal choice. 15 PP would be more useful in long run, especially with Ph's Critical Stream than just mere ~2% damage.
* The Pwr increase Shifta formula for pet is //(Shifta Power / 1000 + 1% * Number of Support Roll) * (Base Pwr * Drink * Tree + Unit Total Pwr + Pet Plus)//
 * Base Pwr is //Character's Base Pwr + Mag + Status Up Skills + S-Ability Stat Up//
 * Pwr Cookies are not affected by Shifta (analogous to how weapon special ability affix isn't included in Shifta)
 * Pet Pwr are not affected by Shifta
 * Harmonizer/Takts are not affected by Shifta
This is according to [https://pso2.swiki.jp/index.php?ダメージ計算 Damage Calculation on PSO2 Capture Wiki] and [https://pso2.swiki.jp/index.php?ペット#shifta Pet Shifta on PSO2 Capture Wiki]

++* Why you seem to focus on PP rather than [[span class="icon stat-tatk"]]TEC /T-ATK[[/span]]?
* I like my PP to hit 300 for Maximum Critical Stream bonus :)
* 400 attack affix is a good spot to stop. You can go to 500 for extra 2% damage or 600 for 4% damage, but it's just few percent for a significantly higher budget.
* Your attack affix is only half-relevant to your output. There's also combos, PP management, frame saving, and your enemy's profile.
* Focusing on PP is cheaper and easier. This makes me able to move on to a new set of unit should the need (e.g. future proofing) and impulse (cheap Add Special Ability items) arises, rather than needlessly stick to my old units.
* All my excess meseta can go to sexy costumes and sexy lobby actions.
