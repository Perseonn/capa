This guide is intended to be a reference for Phantasy Star Online 2's Gathering system. With gathering, you can earn a big amount of Meseta from Daily Order and Yerkes' Client Order. The more characters you have, the better. If you want to know the basic of Gathering, Speak to Sophia on Franca's Cafe.

+ Preface
++ Cuisine Boost Item
All Gathering material have their own type and size, in which a certain cuisine can boost gathering rate. For example, a Naberius Mushroom is a vegetable type and is a medium-size, thus you can use either Tunnels Grilled Mantis Shrimp (boosts vegetable) or Kuron Firefly Squid Pie, (boosts medium size items).

[[div class="alert alert-info"]]
[[span class="fa fa-exclamation-circle"]]@<&nbsp;>@[[/span]] **About Other type:** Other type, has separate boost item for Fishing and Harvesting Material. For example, Surface Warship Model, an other type obtainable by fishing, can be boosted with Shironia Braised Carp, but not Tunnels Spiral Shell Salad. On the contrary, Amduscia Dragon Scales, an other type obtainable by harvesting, can only be boosted with Tunnels Spiral Shell Salad, but not the Shironia Braised Carp.
[[/div]]

[[div class="alert alert-info"]]
[[span class="fa fa-exclamation-circle"]]@<&nbsp;>@[[/span]] **Unlocking Cuisine:** Most of the Cuisines listed here need to be unlocked. You can usually unlock them by gathering the material in the Ingredient 1 on the location where the Ingredient is. For example, the useful Kuron Burdock Bisque is unlocked by fishing in Sanctum and harvesting Sanctum Fiddler Crabs.
[[/div]]
