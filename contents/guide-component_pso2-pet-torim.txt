|pet-name=Trim (//Torim//) / Aero
|pet-description=A pet with high powered PAs, focusing on quality over quantity. They are best suited to pursuit agile enemies with their fast Stab and their double DoT attacks. Their main weakness is their lack of wide area attack, PP inefficiency, low defense, and weakest normal attacks (i.e. not worth for Spirited Parfait). If you can, switch to Trim or Aero during a boss' downtime, such as when Dragon drops its head, Deus showing its core etc. and perform Shout then Spiral to their weak points. In order to maximize their damage, try to land as many full-charged Shout-Spiral as you can.

★14 Trim is more durable with higher HP and Def and less PP usage. ★14 Aero has higher Pwr, upgraded Aero Spriral (wider attack area) and Aero Slash (stronger). There are little reasons to use Trim, so the numbers listed on here are based on Aero.

**Trim and Aero are currently having limited uses as they are outclassed by their glass cannon peers: Shinkuro and Popple; and fellow avian, Rappy.**
