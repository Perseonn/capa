Hero is a scion class that uses [[span class="icon wp-sw"]]Sword[[/span]], [[span class="icon wp-tm"]]TMG[[/span]], and [[span class="icon wp-tl"]]Talis[[/span]]. They have a huge damage boost as long as you can avoid losing 20% of you max HP in 5 second.

[[div class="alert alert-info"]]
[[span class="fa fa-exclamation-circle"]]@<&nbsp;>@[[/span]] **This guide assumes that**:
* You know how PSO2 works in general, as you need 2 level 75 classes to unlock Hero
* You have done the Hero Tutorial
* You know who Stratos (ストラトス) is
* You can amass Excubes
* You can amass Photon Spheres
[[/div]]

++ Hero Mag
Thanks to Hero Mag skill, any points added to a mag's [[span class="icon stat-satk"]]S-ATK[[/span]], [[span class="icon stat-ratk"]]R-ATK[[/span]], and [[span class="icon stat-tatk"]]T-ATK[[/span]] will be distributed to all attacks at 1:1 rate. So if you have 200 [[span class="icon stat-tatk"]]T-ATK[[/span]] mag, you'll also gain 200 [[span class="icon stat-satk"]]S-ATK[[/span]] and 200 [[span class="icon stat-ratk"]]R-ATK[[/span]].

Preferably your mag has this following setup:

+++ About Hero Boost and Hero Counter
Hero Boost gives 80% damage increase, but if you lose 20% of your max HP in 5 seconds (regardless of current HP), you also lose 20% damage, bringing you down to 60%. Hero Boost will build this back by 1% per second, or you can do 1 Hero Counter to bring it back to 80%.

++ [[image img%3Apso2-ui/ui-wp-sw.png class="h2-icon"]] Sword
To know the basics of Sword, please do the Hero Training Quest on Sword. Sword is the easiest weapon to use and used to be the only Hero weapon, delegating the other two to just utility. Now, it's still the most balanced weapon and safest to use. If you want to start using Hero, start with Sword.
