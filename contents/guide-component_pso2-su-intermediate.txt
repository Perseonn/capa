++ Class Combination
[[image info-2-su-subclass.avif  | Summoner Subclass]]

Summoner has 4 viable subclasses: the common Fighter and Phantom, and the more niche Braver and Bouncer:
* SuFi: Most common combination. Best combination if Ph is not yet available
* SuPh: The premier combo for everything. It is stronger than SuFi with great PP regeneration, but requires the character to have 200 PP
* SuBr: Great for beginners, especially players new to Summoner **and** new to PSO2
* SuBo: Has good PP regen and strong against breakable parts, but difficult to use.

All multipliers listed are not counting High Level Bonus, which is a must-take. The "Damage Rating" stated is based on critical builds, whose explanation can be found after this section.


+++ Summoner-Fighter (SuFi)
Recommended for all skill levels. SuFi has the highest stance multiplier and less likely need to switch stance, but offers little compensation once PP runs out and has lowest critical rate (means your base damage will be lower). Despite this, SuFi remains well-rounded and is a good choice towards end-game, especially if Phantom is not available.

++ About Critical Hits
While Su does not have JA, this is offset by pets having 70% minimum damage, as opposed to other weapon's 90% when using a ★13. Adding as much critical as possible allows pet to reach higher average damage, without compensating any aspects (maybe that 10 PP from 2 Spirit Canes).

[[include comp:pso2-manaka
|content=Stack as many critical rate up as you can, it's a shortcut to high damage!]]

[[collapsible show="Show Simplified Calculation" hide="Hide Simplified Calculation"]]
By default, pet's minimum damage is roughly 70%, while Critical hits in PSO2 automatically put your damage to 100%. Thus, without any enhancements, your mean damage using a pet is:

> (100% + 70%) / 2 = 85%

However, at any circumstances, you have at least 5% critical rate, which means 95% of time you deal average 85% damage, while the 5% of the time, you deal 100% of the damage.

> 95% * 85% + 5% * 100% = 85.75%

In other words, without any critical enhancements, averagely, you only do 85.75% damage.

Lets say you are using a Class Stance Critical (25%, assuming either Br or Bo sub) and a Critical Strike Ring Tech (20%), you will have a total of 50% Critical Rate (5% + 25% + 20%). Ignoring Critical Damage bonus from ring, your average damage will be:

> 50% * 85% + 50% * 100% = 92.5%

By boosting your critical rate to 50%, you gain 7.87% (relative to 85.75%) more damage.

What if you use all the critical option available without outside aid? Assuming Br or Bo sub, you already have 50% critical with ring (5% base + 25% stance + 20% ring). Add 14% from 2 Critical Candy Sticks and 20% from 2 Support Rolls. This will add up to 84% critical rate. Your average damage will be:

> 16% * 85% + 84% * 100% = 97.6%

You are almost about 14.82% (relative to 85.75%) more powerful than a non-critical build.

Example, a SuBr uses Deadly Parfait and maxed Critical Strike Tech ring: 84% critical rate and 103% * 110% = 113.3% damage during a critical hit (3% from Ring, 10% from Deadly Parfait). Their average damage will be:

> 16% * 85% + 84% * 113.3% = 108.77%

At more than 80% critical rate, the Deadly Parfait is more powerful than Super Complete Parfait (The damage rate with Super Complete Parfait alone will be 108.13%). And, with outside aid like Shifta Critical and Critical Field, pretty much 100% critical is guaranteed, bumping the overall damage to 113.3%

+++ Glossary

||~ Terms ||~ Description ||
|| DPS Rating || Rating of Damage per second, calculated roughly by damage divided by frame, divided by average damage of Pet PAs. This is solely used to determine which pet attack is more powerful when used over and over. It does not mean that it is the most efficient attack. ||
|| Recall PA || A Photon Arts (PA) that returns pet to your side and stops it from using further attack. This will pause the effect of Near Auto Target ring. Unless noted, pet will perform the move first before returning. ||

A DoT skill will sucessfully deliver damage if the first hit lands, even if the part is hidden or the pet got stunned. However, it will cancel if the affected part is destroyed, or pet is unsummoned. ||
